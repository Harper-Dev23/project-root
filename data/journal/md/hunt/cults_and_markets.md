# Cults and Markets

The regions have their false gods, and the false gods have followers. A **cult band** camps on the map like a pack of beasts: walk onto it and it fights. Some bands lie hidden in ambush. Cultists wear a full set of armour, and when they fall, their chest piece always drops, sometimes with one more piece.

In the Reeds of Gethsemane, two cults have questlines of their own, in the Quest Log's **Regions** tab: the **Drowned Choir**, who sing to Yar'galeth, and the **Temple of the Gill**, who feed Dagon. A cult's line opens the first time you fight one of its bands, or when Elder Varek hears the Vowback Crocodile is dead.

## Parley

Once a cult knows you, from its questline's first step, its bands will talk instead of fight. They only talk when **you found them**: you walked onto their camp, or you knew they were there. A band that ambushes you never talks.

A parley is a trade. The band names its price, paid from your pack, and pays in **Sin Tickets**:

- the **Drowned Choir** want **3 fish**, for **2 Sin Tickets**;
- the **Temple of the Gill** want **2 lean game**, for **2 Sin Tickets**.

Every trade also earns you a little standing with their god. If you cannot pay, you fight or you flee.

## Sin Tickets

Sin Tickets are the cults' currency. You earn them from parleys and from cult questlines, and you spend them only at the cults' black markets.

## The black markets

Each cult's questline ends by opening its market. Once it is open, the market turns up on about **4 hunts in 10** in its regions, marked on the map.

- **The Tithe-Boat** (Drowned Choir): the Reeds and the Bay of Solace.
- **The Gill Market** (Temple of the Gill): the Reeds.

Both sell:

- **The Cult Gamble**, 1 Sin Ticket: a random piece of armour, uncommon to epic (30 / 45 / 25 in 100). One in a hundred comes up **Corrupted**.
- **Tinctures**, 2 Sin Tickets each. *Red Breath* restores half the drinker's HP and costs some MP. *Deep Well* restores half their MP and costs some HP. Each is a bonus action in combat, and only the hunter who drinks it is affected.

The Gill Market also sells **Smuggled Parts**, 3 Sin Tickets: a part from one of the region's beasts, rare or better.

## Corrupted gear

A Corrupted piece of armour is stronger than its kind should be, spread over its own stats, and carries one malus on a stat it does not have. The higher the base, the bigger both are.

## Divinity Tickets

Prophet shrines sometimes pay in **Divinity Tickets**. Nothing spends them yet; more uses are coming.
