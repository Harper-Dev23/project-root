# Bosses and Historic Items

## Finding a boss

A region's bosses are not found by wandering. Each one has its own **questline** in the Quest Log's **Regions** tab. While you are on one of its steps, every hunt in that region holds what the step needs, marked in violet on the map -- some only stir at night. At the end of the questline your tribe gives you a **boss hunt plan**, free.

## Omens

Everything you do in a region fills its **Omen meter**: objectives most of all, then fights, the apex, and events seen through. The meter shows on the Hunt screen when you choose the region. Once you know a boss, a full meter buys another of its boss hunt plans -- you choose which boss. Plans can be saved up.

A boss plan is **not** used up when you depart. It is used up once you fight the boss, win or lose.

## The lair

A boss hunt puts the boss in its **lair**, deep in the map and marked from the start. Stepping onto the lair shows a warning first: it is a boss fight, and you can camp and ready the party before you go in. With friends, only the host can lead the party in.

## Historic items

A **Historic item** is one of a kind in the realm. A region's boss keeps one, and gives it up on the kill while it is still in the wild; some are found by chance instead, never guaranteed. Every boss kill is worth it beyond the Historic item -- its own parts, or gear off its fallen, and something else in the item's place when someone already holds it.

Historic items roll their stats within a range. A Historic item can be **returned** at your lodge's shrine: it goes back to where it came from, and can be won again, with new rolls.
