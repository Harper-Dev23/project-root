# When the Party Falls

A hunter knocked out in a fight is only down. If the whole party goes down, the hunt is **wiped**, and what that costs depends on the region. Every region has one of three rules, shown on the Hunt screen.

## Sheltered

Nobody dies. The party is carried back to camp, the fallen standing at 1 HP, and the pack comes home: what you brought and what you found. **Both starting coasts, the Reeds of Gethsemane and the Bay of Solace, are Sheltered.**

## Watched

The region's prophet is watching. The fallen go to the **Slain**, and the pack is lost. Equipped gear stays with its hunter.

At the moment of the wipe, the prophet may **speak for** some of your hunters, paid for with your Bond's standing with their house. The ones spoken for stand up at 1 HP, and the hunt goes on from the nearest way out.

Back in camp, a Slain hunter can come back:

- **Intercession**, at your lodge's shrine, for a hunter who fell in the lands of the house your tribe follows. It is immediate, and costs **10 × their level** in Bond standing with that house.
- **The lesser rite**, for any Watched death. It takes **2 + their level** days (days pass only while you hunt) and **5 × their level** Hunt Tickets. The hunter waits on the Slain roster until it is done.

## Forsaken

The region belongs to a false god. The fallen go to the Slain and the pack is lost, and neither intercession nor the rite can bring them back. Only the **false god** can, at once, and its price is standing with it, hidden from you, plus Bond standing with the house your tribe follows. That Bond can go below zero. A hunter you let go is gone for good.

## Hunting together

On a shared hunt, each player's own hunters follow the region's rule in their own save: see [[Hunting Together]].
