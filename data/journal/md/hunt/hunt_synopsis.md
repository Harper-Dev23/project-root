# Hunt Synopsis

Quick reference for a Hunt, start to finish:

1. **Choose a region** on the Hunt screen, pack Rations for extra supplies, and pick a Hunt Plan -- or go on the free Basic Hunt Plan. The plan decides the map's size and what you are there to do.
2. **Depart**, alone or with friends. You arrive at the entry of a hex map you have never seen; most of it is dark until you walk close.
3. **Explore.** Every step costs supplies and time, more in marsh and bog than on grass. Day turns to night and back. You see what is near, sense what is further off, and can stop to **Scout** something you only half made out.
4. **Meet what lives there.** Walk onto a pack and the fight begins. Some packs wander, and hunters among them will come for you. Events wait on their own tiles.
5. **Do the plan's objective**, marked on the map from the start where it has a place.
6. **Camp, eat and forage** to keep the party standing and fed.
7. **Leave** from the entry or a Waystone. A clean exit with the objective done pays the completion reward; whatever is in the pack comes home.

See [[The Sacred Hunt]] for the numbers, and [[Bosses and Historic Items]] for what waits deepest in a region.
