# Hunt Synopsis

Quick reference for a Hunt, start to finish:

1. **Choose a location** from the map, then set your loadout — spend Hunt Tickets for extra supplies, and pick a Hunt Plan if one's available.
2. **Depart.** Supplies start draining, and day/night and weather begin shifting your odds.
3. **Advance.** Each turn rolls either a fight (a Beast or a Cultist) or an Event (a choice, a stat check, or a riddle) — Investigate it before you can move on.
4. **Resolve it.** Fights go to the Combat Pit and can't be fled once engaged; Events lock you in until you finish them. Either way you earn Hunt Points, and sometimes XP or HP effects.
5. **Return to Camp** once supplies run out — your party is restored to full and your Hunt Points are banked.
