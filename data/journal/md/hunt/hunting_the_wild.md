# Hunting the Wild

## Tempers

Every beast family has a **temper**, and it decides what the beast does about you. Once you have identified a beast, its temper shows when you select its tile.

- **Predators** hunt. A wandering predator that notices you within **2 steps** comes for you, and one you flee from follows. They are also drawn to fresh kills (see *Blood in the water*). In the Reeds: crocodiles and marsh vipers.
- **Territorial** beasts fight for their ground. Step onto one and it fights; flee and it chases only while you stay within **2 steps** of where it stood. In the Reeds: swamp crabs and snapping turtles.
- **Timid** beasts never hunt. Walk onto one and it **scatters** to a free tile nearby instead of fighting, unless it is cornered. To fight one, choose **Hunt it**. In the Reeds: bog frogs, nutria, marsh bats and scarlet ibis.

An apex, the beasts your plan sends you after, and a quest's beast are never so easy: they stand and fight, and they hunt you after a flee, whatever their temper.

## Prophet marks

Each beast pack wears a ring on the map, and its name in a fight shows the same colour.

- **Marked (gold)**: claimed by the prophet who watches the region. Killing a marked pack earns that prophet's **favor** on the hunt: 1, 2, 3 or 5 for each yearling, grown, prime or great beast in it.
- **Unmarked (silver)**: no prophet's. Killing one earns no favor, and the region's false god always takes note.
- **Corrupted (purple)**: an unmarked beast that has stood in blight for a full day turns. Corrupted beasts are hard to see from a distance and earn no favor.

The beasts your plan sends you after are always marked.

## Vigils

Some events ask you to keep a **vigil** for the region's prophet, for the rest of the hunt. Under a vigil:

- killing an **unmarked** beast costs **3** standing with that prophet's house, unless it dies on blight: the prophet forgives a kill on blighted ground;
- an unmarked beast you flee from is let go instead of following you.

The fight's opening line tells you a pack's mark, and what killing it would cost under the vigil.

## Blight

Blight is dead, corrupting ground. It spreads from a **blight source**, one ring further every day for as long as the source lives, and any unmarked beast left standing in it turns Corrupted.

Stand on a blighted tile and **Cleanse** it (**2** time) to give it back its old ground. Cleanse the source's own tile to **destroy the source**: its spread stops, and its blight recedes a ring a day. Cleansing tiles around a living source only buys time, because they blight again at the next day.

Some plans bring extra blight patches with them.

## Waiting

You can **Wait** without moving: a short wait (about one move's time), or until dark, or until dawn. The world goes on while you wait, so packs move and night events stir. Waiting costs supplies at a camp's rate and recovers nothing. A pack that reaches you has to find you first, as it would a camp, and if it does you are ambushed.

## Blood in the water

A fresh kill draws attention. After a beast fight, the nearest wandering **predator** within **4 steps**, in the same part of the map, may catch the scent and come to the kill. Only one comes at a time, and none while something is already hunting you. You are told when something has caught the scent, but not how long you have: a quick harvest is usually safe, and a long one may not be.
