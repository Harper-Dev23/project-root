# Hunting Together

A hunt can be shared. One player **hosts** from the Hunt Gate, choosing the region and the plan, and friends join the lobby with their own hunters, up to a full party between you. Each player places their own hunters in the formation, and nobody can move anyone else's.

## Packing

- **Rations**: every player can pledge Rations to the shared pack. What is left at the end is split back by what each of you brought.
- **Your own pack**: everything else you bring (draughts, tinctures, spare gear) goes in your own pack, which only you use. Guests pack in the lobby; the host packs on the Hunt screen. Your camp bag stays in camp, as it does solo.

## On the map

**Anyone can move the party.** The host does everything else: forage, fish, camp, wait, choose at events, start and flee fights, and lead the party into a boss's lair.

Fights are shared. Each player commands their own hunters on their own turns, and gear you equip on the map is what your hunter wears in the next fight.

## What you find

Everything the party finds, from loot and harvested parts to boss chests and event rewards, is copied into **every player's own pack** as soon as it turns up. You can equip or use your copy mid-hunt, just like solo. Fresh food stays in the shared pack, where the party eats from it and pays events with it.

## Coming home

When the hunt ends, every player takes home their own share:

- **Hunt Points** and the plan's completion reward and bonus objectives: in full, to everyone.
- **Experience**: each fight's XP is split over the whole party, and each player's hunters get their share.
- **Finds**: your own pack comes home with your copies in it.
- **Standing and the calendar**: favor, standing and every day that passed count in every save.
- **Quest progress**: steps done on the hunt count for everyone, and show in your Quest Log while the hunt is still on.

A wipe follows the region's rule for each player's own hunters: see [[When the Party Falls]].

## Leaving early

A guest can leave at any time and takes a **clean exit**: the finds so far, and the completion reward only if the objective was already done. If the host drops, the hunt waits a while for them to come back; if they do not, every guest takes a clean exit from where the hunt last stood.
