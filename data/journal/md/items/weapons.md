# Weapons

Crude, common-rarity weapons are the starting baseline at Ironbinder's Stand — a dagger, swords (one- and two-handed), a spear, whip, shield, sling, bow, gun, staff, wand, and both a mace and axe in two-handed form. None carry bonus affixes; they're meant to be replaced.

Beyond that baseline, weapons can roll uncommon, rare, or epic with bonus affixes layered on — elemental damage, lifesteal, and other combat modifiers. Unique weapons (soulbound, often tribe- or boss-tied) skip the random roll entirely and carry one fixed, always-on bonus instead.

See *Weapon Types and Handedness* under Systems for which weapon types occupy one hand versus two.
