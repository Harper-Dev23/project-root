# Weapons

Crude, common-rarity weapons are the starting baseline at Ironbinder's Stand — a dagger, swords (one- and two-handed), a spear, whip, shield, sling, bow, gun, staff, wand, and both a mace and axe in two-handed form. None carry bonus affixes; they're meant to be replaced.

Beyond that baseline, weapons can roll uncommon, rare, or epic with bonus affixes layered on — elemental damage, lifesteal, and other combat modifiers. Unique weapons (soulbound, often tribe- or boss-tied) skip the random roll entirely and carry one fixed, always-on bonus instead.

## Base types

Every weapon sits on one of three bases, and the base is named in the item itself:

| Base | Damage | Where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| **Crude** | the baseline | vendors, and any Hunt Ticket gamble |
| **Hardened** | about 20% more on both ends | Reckoning tiers, and the bone pile's Marked buttons |
| **Ancestral** | about 45% more | reserved for later — nothing in the pit fields one yet |

The base is multiplied by everything else on the weapon, so the same *Merciless* roll is worth meaningfully more on a Hardened blade than a Crude one. Two weapons with identical modifiers are not the same weapon.

**Bone** is not a fourth base. It is an overlay on whatever base was already going to drop — see [[Historic (Renown) Items]].

See *Weapon Types and Handedness* under Systems for which weapon types occupy one hand versus two.
