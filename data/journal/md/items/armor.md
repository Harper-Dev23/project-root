# Armor

Armor covers six slots — helm, chest, legs, gloves, boots, and amulet. Watershade's starting stock is simple, common-rarity gear, each piece leaning toward a specific stat (STR, DEX, INT, CON, WIS), so a build can gear toward what it actually uses.

Amulets are where tribe identity shows up directly: each of the four tribes has its own uncommon, unique pendants with a single fixed bonus (no random rolling) — Elseth's convert one damage type into another, Styx grants initiative/cooldown/shield effects, and Zafaar's disorient enemies. Higher-rarity armor elsewhere rolls the same affix system weapons use — more affixes at higher rarity, fewer (none) at common.
