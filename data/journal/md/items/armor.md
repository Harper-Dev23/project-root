# Armor

Armor covers **seven slots** -- head, chest, legs, gloves, boots, **amulet** and **ring**. Watershade's starting stock is simple common-rarity gear, each piece leaning toward a specific attribute (STR, DEX, INT, CON, WIS), so a build can gear toward what it actually uses.

Above common, armor rolls from the same modifier system weapons use -- more modifiers at higher rarity. Beyond raw stats, armor modifiers are where you find weakness-buildup bonuses (physical, elemental and necrotic), healing done, mana per turn and Resilience.

## Base types

Armour has two bases. **Simple** is the Watershade baseline. **Fitted** carries **one more stat on every slot** -- two on head, legs, gloves and boots, three on chest -- before a single modifier is rolled. Fitted pieces come from the Reckoning tiers and from the bone pile's Marked buttons; see [[Item Level & Base Types]].

## Tribe jewellery

Amulets and rings are where tribe identity shows up directly. Each of the four tribes has **three amulets and three rings** -- uncommon, unique, and carrying a single fixed effect rather than rolled affixes.

- **Elseth** -- conversion and procs. Their amulets convert one damage type into another (physical to elemental, physical to necrotic, elemental to necrotic); their rings proc extra damage of a given type.
- **Styx** -- tempo and protection. Initiative, cooldown reduction and a one-time shield on their amulets; healing and recovery effects on their rings.
- **Zafaar** -- pressure. Their amulets drive Disorient, Expose and Lacerate buildup; their rings gamble on damage, doubling it or halving what comes back.
- **Le'sse** -- elemental craft. Fire, cold and lightning amulets, and rings that override the damage type of what you swing.

## Rarity

Higher rarity means more rolled affixes -- see [[Items & Equipment]] for the full table. Unique items like tribe jewellery ignore that entirely and carry their one fixed bonus instead.
