# Historic (Renown) Items

Certain weapons and armor can gain **Renown** and become "Historic." Once Historic, an item grows with the character or tribe.

**Core traits**
- **Soulbound** - cannot be permanently lost; returns to inventory if destroyed during a hunt.
- **Questlines** - upgrade paths tied to element, origin, tribe, or corruption themes.
- **Legacy growth** - gains permanent strength or properties rather than being replaced.
- **History log** - records owners, deeds, and milestones.

**Baseline triggers** include usage milestones, notable victories, survival across hunts, and special origins such as bosses, shrines, vendors, or quests. Progression paths may follow elemental, tribal, or corruption alignments with unique rewards.

## Bone

Bone is the oldest kind of Renown, and the only one that is *found* rather than earned. Roughly one weapon in a hundred pulled from the bone pile comes up Bone.

It is **not a base type of its own**. It is an overlay on whatever base was already going to drop, adding **20% damage before any other modifier** -- which means every percentage on the weapon multiplies the bone bonus too. Because it sits on top of an ordinary base, Bone can appear on any of them; a Bone weapon pulled from a Marked roll is built on a Hardened base.

Every one of them reads simply *Bone Dagger*, *Bone War Mace*. The Base Tier line on the item is what separates them.

**Severing** is the other origin, and it is earned rather than dropped: a Severing Chant used in combat cuts the soul-bond on a piece an enemy is wearing, turning gear that would never have dropped into ordinary loot. A severed item is renamed for it -- *Light Cap* becomes *Severed Cap*.

Natural weapons are exempt from all of this. A beast's fangs and talons are part of the animal, cannot be looted, and cannot be severed.
