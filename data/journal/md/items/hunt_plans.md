# Hunt Plans

A Hunt Plan is chosen on the Hunt screen before you depart, and it shapes the whole trip: the map's **size**, the **objective** you are there to complete, and any **bonus objectives** and modifiers it rolled. Every plan has an **item level**, a **tier** set by that level, and modifiers rolled like the affixes on gear.

A plan is used up when you leave camp, whether you finish its objective or not. Two kinds are not: the Basic Hunt Plan, and a boss plan (see below).

## Objectives

Each plan names one primary objective. Where it has a place on the map, that place is marked from the start.

- **Scout**: reveal every marked site (2 / 3 / 4 sites on a small / medium / large map).
- **Cull**: kill a number of beasts of one native family (4 / 6 / 8). The plan's own quarry is always Marked, and stands and fights.
- **Apex**: kill the region's apex beast.
- **Retrieve**: take the item from its site and carry it to an exit.
- **Commune**: reach the region's shrine and see it through.
- **Boss**: kill a boss in its lair. Only a boss plan carries this one; see [[Bosses and Historic Items]].

A **clean exit** with the objective done pays the completion reward: **20 / 35 / 50 Hunt Points** by map size, raised by the plan's prefixes and tier, and an XP pool for the party.

## The Basic Hunt Plan

You never go out without a plan. The **Basic Hunt Plan** is free, never runs out, and is what you take when you choose nothing else: a small map to scout, with no modifiers.

## Buying plans

The **Greenhollow Satchel** on Vendor Row sells three plans a day, for Hunt Tickets, **one ticket per item level**. It stocks plans up to your party's highest level, with at least one at that level. The stock changes once a day, and days pass while you hunt. Each plan on the table sells once.

Small maps are on sale from the start. **Medium** maps open once you have reported the Vowback Crocodile to Elder Varek, and **large** maps once you have killed your first boss.

Some quest steps hand you a plan as a reward. It works like a bought one, and is used up when you depart.

## Boss plans

A boss plan names one boss. It is not used up when you depart: only once you fight that boss, win or lose. You earn them from a boss's questline and from a region's full Omen meter.

## Tiers

A plan's tier comes from its item level; it is never rolled. A higher tier is always the better plan, however its modifiers turn out.

- **Tier I**: item levels 1 to 4.
- **Tier II**: item levels 5 to 7, with a larger completion reward.
- **Tier III**: item levels 8 to 10, with a larger completion reward still, and one bonus objective always on the plan.

The tier shows in the plan's name: a *Hunt Plan III* is Tier III.

## Modifiers

Rarity decides how many modifiers a plan rolls, exactly as it does on gear, and item level decides how strong they can be. Every modifier comes in five tiers, so even a level 1 plan rolls something.

- **Prefixes are what the hunt demands**: more beasts, harsher weather, poorer ground, extra blight. Each one also raises the completion reward.
- **Suffixes are your party's edge**: lighter travel, better loot, extra Rations in the pack, more experience or Hunt Points.

## Bonus objectives

A plan can carry a **bonus objective**: something extra to achieve on the hunt, such as revealing most of the map, bringing home a trophy, or getting through every fight with no one knocked out. At most one rolls as a prefix, and a Tier III plan adds a second, different one. The harder objectives only appear on higher-level plans. Each one you finish pays on a clean exit, on top of the completion reward.

The full list of modifiers, their tiers and numbers, and every bonus objective is in [[Affix Reference (In Depth)]].
