# Hunt Plans

A Hunt Plan is chosen on the Hunt screen before you depart, and it shapes the whole trip. Every plan has an **item level**, a **tier** set by that level, and modifiers rolled like the affixes on gear. A general plan is used up when you leave camp.

## The Basic Hunt Plan

You never go out without a plan. The **Basic Hunt Plan** is free, never runs out, and is what you take when you choose nothing else. It is a small map to scout, with no modifiers.

## Buying plans

The **Greenhollow Satchel** on Vendor Row sells plans for Hunt Tickets, **one ticket per item level**. It stocks plans up to your party's highest level, with at least one at that level. The stock changes once a day, and days pass while you hunt. Each plan on the table sells once.

## Tiers

A plan's tier comes from its item level; it is never rolled. A higher tier is always the better plan, however its modifiers turn out.

- **Tier I** — item levels 1–4.
- **Tier II** — item levels 5–7, with a larger completion reward.
- **Tier III** — item levels 8–10, with a larger completion reward still, and one bonus objective always on the plan.

The tier shows in the plan's name: a *Hunt Plan III* is Tier III.

## Modifiers

Rarity decides how many modifiers a plan rolls, exactly as it does on gear, and item level decides how strong they can be. Every modifier comes in five tiers, so even a level 1 plan rolls something.

- **Prefixes are what the hunt demands** — more beasts, harsher weather, poorer ground. Each one also raises the completion reward.
- **Suffixes are your party's edge** — lighter travel, better loot, extra Rations in the pack, more experience or Hunt Points.

Some modifiers are marked *no effect yet*. They roll and show on the plan now, but the parts of the hunt they change are still being built. The completion reward and bonus objectives are among them.

## Bonus objectives

A plan can carry a **bonus objective**: something extra to achieve on the hunt, such as revealing most of the map, bringing home a trophy, or getting through every fight with no one knocked out. At most one rolls as a prefix, and a Tier III plan adds a second, different one. The harder objectives only appear on higher-level plans.

The full list of modifiers, their tiers and numbers, and every bonus objective is in [[Affix Reference (In Depth)]].
