# Hunt Plans

A Hunt Plan is a loadout item chosen before departing on a Hunt, picked up free from Wayfinder's Cache at the vendor row. It carries no fixed stats of its own — instead, like weapons and armor, it rolls affixes scaled to its rarity, and those affixes become active modifiers on the Hunt itself (things like supply efficiency or encounter odds) once you depart.

A chosen Hunt Plan is consumed on departure — it's a one-trip loadout choice, not a permanent piece of gear.
