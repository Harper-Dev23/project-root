# Items & Equipment

Everything a Hunter can carry falls into a few types: **weapons**, **armor**, **relics**, **consumables**, and **Hunt Plans**. Most come in four rarity tiers — common, uncommon, rare, and epic — and higher rarity means more rolled affixes (bonus stats):

- **Common** — no affixes. A plain, reliable baseline.
- **Uncommon** — 1–2 affixes.
- **Rare** — exactly 3 affixes.
- **Epic** — 4 affixes (2 prefixes + 2 suffixes, always paired).

Some items break this pattern entirely: **unique** items (like tribe pendants) come with a single fixed bonus instead of rolling from the random pool, and **soulbound** items (like the Waystone Shard) can never be sold or lost.

## Two hidden numbers

Rarity is only half the story. Every piece also carries an **item level** and a **base type**, neither of which is a stat:

- **Item level** decides which *tiers* of modifier were allowed to roll. It is a floor and never a ceiling — a high item level does not stop weak modifiers appearing, it only adds better ones on top.
- **Base type** is the item underneath the modifiers. Weapons run **Crude → Hardened → Ancestral** and armour runs **Simple → Fitted**; a higher base carries more raw damage, or one more stat, *before* any percentage is applied. Every percent on the item therefore multiplies a bigger number.

Both are stamped when the item is made and never change. [[Item Level & Base Types]] has the gating tables.

Every modifier, its tier and its exact roll range is listed in [[Affix Reference (In Depth)]].
