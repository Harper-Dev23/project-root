# Items & Equipment

Everything a Hunter can carry falls into a few types: **weapons**, **armor**, **relics**, **consumables**, and **Hunt Plans**. Most come in four rarity tiers — common, uncommon, rare, and epic — and higher rarity means more rolled affixes (bonus stats):

- **Common** — no affixes. A plain, reliable baseline.
- **Uncommon** — 1–2 affixes.
- **Rare** — exactly 3 affixes.
- **Epic** — 4 affixes (2 prefixes + 2 suffixes, always paired).

Some items break this pattern entirely: **unique** items (like tribe pendants) come with a single fixed bonus instead of rolling from the random pool, and **soulbound** items (like the Waystone Shard) can never be sold or lost.

See also: *Weapon Types and Handedness* and *Historic (Renown) Items* under Systems for how weapon slots and long-term item growth work.
