# Nahlia Vey

Nahlia Vey — "the Contract Flame" — is Elseth's tribal vendor and spiritual broker at Camp Nehemiah, renowned for her control over divine commerce. Never seen without her obsidian register, every gesture from her is the beginning of a deal. "Gold glitters," she warns. "Debt glows." Cool, transactional, and unnervingly precise, she demands respectful conduct and true naming before trade.

Elseth Hunters can trade Tribe Tickets at her stall for the tribe's unique pendants — only those who've pledged to Elseth may buy from her; she turns everyone else away without ceremony.
