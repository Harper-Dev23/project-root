# The Seers

The Seers — "those who do not blink" — are a silent collective bound to the Sacred Hunt, residing in the Seers' Tent at Camp Nehemiah. Shrouded and indistinct, felt more than seen, they bear unblinking witness to the cycle of trial, death, and transformation, but never intervene in it.

"The Hunt remembers what the living forget," they're said to communicate without words, in felt thought rather than speech. "We are its eyes when yours close." A Hunter is sent to them once Samuel Mourne judges they're ready — the Seers have watched since the day they arrived.
