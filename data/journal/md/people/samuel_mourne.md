# Samuel Mourne

Samuel Mourne — known in older records as "the Scarred Truthsayer" — is a lorekeeper stationed at Camp Nehemiah, found near the Mourne Hut. He watches newcomers through their early training and steps in once they've proven themselves, explaining what the tribes don't say openly: the Sacred Hunt is more than sport, and each Prophet is a divine being wearing the shape of a great beast — ancient, aware, and older than anything the tribes call a god.

He's the one who first sends a Hunter to attune to the waystone network, and the one they return to once they have. Cryptic and reverent, his caution isn't a flaw — survivors of the Ruins of Exodus tend to choose their words carefully.
