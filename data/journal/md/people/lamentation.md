# Lamentation — The Crimson Whisper

A colossal, ancient serpent, her scales the shade of drowned blood, her movements silent as dustfall. She coils through the [[Caverns of Lament]] — a sacred ossuary now warped by her presence. Her hiss echoes as agonic whispers, often mistaken for memory or prophecy, until too late.

*"It never ends. You only stop struggling."*

## Disposition

Patient, cold, and reverent in her cruelty. She considers herself a sacred warning rather than a predator. Her origin is disputed: born from Prophet silence, some say, or from the broken ribs of an elder False God.

She exists to preserve the silence of the caverns, and to convert all sound into memoryless weeping.

## What She Offers

Agonic wisdom, prophetic misdirection, and bone-bound secrets — at a price. What she wants is quiet interlopers, despair-filled offerings, and the denial of joy.

## Approaching Her

She cannot be reached from camp; she exists only in cautionary whispers from [[The Seers]] and older hunters. Disturbed bones, echoes of laughter, and relics of joy or fire all provoke her — she reacts violently to warmth and song.

In vision she appears as a massive crimson coil that weeps where it breathes, her eyes reflecting things you have not yet done.

## Whispers

The cavern's echo is her voice multiplied; killing her may silence the region permanently. She may once have been a Prophet's failed companion, cast into the deep with a sacred name she can no longer recall.
