# Augustine — The Deep Listener

A semi-divine oracle hippo, known to few and trusted by fewer. Augustine dwells in the moss-choked tributaries east of [[Lake Genesis]], where fog lies like a shroud and the water mirrors stars that do not exist. His presence halts all lesser life — insects still, birds mute, frogs gone.

He is not a Prophet, nor a False God, but something deeper: a listener of eternal memory, the record of the drowned world. *"He remembers the shape of truth before it was bound to speech."*

## Disposition

Solemn, cryptic, and unbothered by mortal fear or flattery. He was first recorded near the Fourth Rebirth Cycle of Behel'ith, though his origin is unknown. He exists to witness, to retain, and to answer only when truly asked.

## What He Offers

- Symbolic truths about the Prophets and their forms
- The hidden meaning of divine relics and sacred geography
- Veiled knowledge of False God movements and moods

He needs nothing material — only respectful address.

## Approaching Him

He must be addressed by name, and will answer exactly three questions per person. Hunters who have sat with him describe the silence afterward as heavier than the answers themselves.

## Whispers

Some hold that he is older than [[Lake Genesis]] itself — that he predates the divine division entirely. Others say he is the unconscious memory of a forgotten Prophet, made flesh and left to remember on their behalf.
