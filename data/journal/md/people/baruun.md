# Baruun

Baruun — "the Emberbound" — is Zafaar's tribal vendor and sacred smith at Camp Nehemiah, known for forging weapons in sleep and reshaping doubt into flame. Towering, scarred, and calm-eyed, they offer strength but demand conviction: "The world bends to heat. Will you?"

Zafaar Hunters can trade Tribe Tickets at Baruun's stall for the tribe's unique pendants — only those who've pledged to Zafaar may buy from them; anyone else gets turned away.
