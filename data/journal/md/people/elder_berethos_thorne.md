# Elder Berethos Thorne

Elder Berethos Thorne — "the Flamebound Warden" — is the presiding spiritual authority at Camp Nehemiah and the primary interpreter of the Sacred Hunt. Scarred and sacred-branded, he speaks with iron certainty at the ritual firepit, where the flames are said to reflect prophetic truth back at him.

He's the one who walks a Hunter through each stage of training, explains the laws and stakes of the Hunt, and presides over the choice of tribe allegiance. "We do not hunt for sport," he holds — "we hunt to remember. To fail is to forget who we are." Steady, didactic, and fiercely protective of doctrine, he has no patience for theological drift.
