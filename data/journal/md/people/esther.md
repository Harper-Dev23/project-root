# Esther — Guardian of the Springs of Sheol

A barefoot woman cloaked in frost, silent by choice, anchored at the boiling edge of the [[Springs of Sheol]]. Her presence alone wards off the manifestation of False Gods. She watches, she judges, and she allows passage only to those who understand the cost of memory.

*"Speak not your name. Offer your forgetting."*

## Disposition

Severe, silent, soul-worn. Her origin is unknown — possibly a mortal remnant of the first Rebirth cycle. What is clear is her purpose: to guard what sleeps beneath, and to ensure no unworthy soul awakens [[Molech]].

## What She Offers

Passage into the [[Springs of Sheol]] — for those willing to sacrifice memory. She asks no gold. The offering she requires is of the self.

## Approaching Her

She is never seen in camp; her domain is absolute. Speaking a full name aloud in her presence, or violating the sanctity of stillness, ends an audience immediately. In vision she appears as a mirrored silhouette — your own face, silent and unblinking.

## Whispers

She may be the sole survivor of the original covenant that bound [[Molech]]. Some hold she is not alive in any conventional sense, and that it is her memory alone sustaining the veil over the springs.

Hunters trade three stories about her: one sought her blessing and returned with no memory of his children; a cult of false fire tried to draw her into speech and melted without flame; and her shadow, they say, speaks when she does not — and it remembers your name.
