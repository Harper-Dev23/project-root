# Vashra Lyle

Vashra Lyle — "the Fangkeeper" — is Styx's tribal vendor and sacred item broker at Camp Nehemiah, known for bartering in whispers and bones. Often found tending an altar of ash and fang near the camp's edge, where no birds sing and beasts sit in still silence, they speak rarely — "spirits do not forget the ones who feed them" — unless the spirits stir.

Styx Hunters can trade Tribe Tickets at Vashra's stall for the tribe's unique pendants — only those who've pledged to Styx may buy from them; everyone else is turned away.
