# Aivorel

Aivorel — "the Thornsinger" — is Le'sse's tribal vendor at Camp Nehemiah, a silent artisan whose tools are thorns, moonlight, and memory. Poised and soft-spoken, they're often seen weaving spells into vines or conversing with petals that seem to remember names. "Growth is neither gentle nor cruel," they say. "It simply knows what must be."

Le'sse Hunters can trade Tribe Tickets at Aivorel's stall for the tribe's unique pendants — only those who've pledged to Le'sse may buy from them; everyone else is turned away with a quiet word about trading "only with our own."
