# Mordecai — The Winter Stag

A towering white stag with antlers of curling frost and eyes that reflect starlight through falling snow. He is always near [[Esther]] — her guardian and her echo. He does not speak, yet his breath freezes false life where it stands.

*"He runs the perimeter of the sacred. Not to chase you out, but to see if you return."*

## Disposition

Watchful, silent, mournfully present. His origin is unknown, though many count him a holy beast. His motivation is singular: to guard Esther. Nothing else matters.

## What He Offers

Guidance to Esther's path — rarely, and only if the snow whispers your name. He needs nothing. He remembers.

## Approaching Him

He appears only at the blizzard's edge. If Mordecai ever walks into camp, something has been broken. Threats to Esther provoke him, as do lies told beneath sacred stars. In vision, his antlers spiral upward into a sky you do not recognize.

## Whispers

He carries scars from ancient False Gods — symbols that glow when one draws near. A Prophet is said to have ridden him once, though that Prophet was never seen again.

It is held that his death would mark the end of the final mercy, and that the [[Springs of Sheol]] would open soon after.
