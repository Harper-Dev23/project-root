# Yar'galeth – The Submerged Mouth

*Major False God of drowned words, forgotten oaths, and echoes without origin*

## Domain & Distortion

Yar'galeth is the liar in the undertow — a False God whose truths come bubbling up from the deepest places, beautiful but unmoored from any foundation. It speaks not lies, but orphaned revelations, unclaimed by time, context, or meaning. _"What you hear beneath the waves is always true. That is the danger."_

It offers prophetic clarity with no grounding. Followers are driven mad not by falsity, but by too many truths all at once, none of which they can act upon. It tempts through whispered prophecy in dreams, answers to unasked questions, or glimpses of futures stolen from others.

## Manifestation & Influence

It takes the form of a rotted sea beast with a throat that never closes, its body coiled with half-formed mouths and unblinking eyes. Its signs include bubbles surfacing in still water where there should be none, tongues of salt forming runes on the skin, and prophetic texts reading backwards or upside down. Cultists chant while submerged, breathing truths until they drown; relics drip seawater and cannot be dried; victims hear words spoken as if from within their own lungs.

## Known Heresies & Cults

The **Heresy of the Unmoored Prophecy** holds that all prophecy is valid regardless of context, leading to contradictory and destructive actions. Its cult, **The Drowned Choir**, operates through dreams and vision-trances, converting scholars, prophets, or visionaries who sought too much.

## Prophet Interference

Yar'galeth frequently entangles itself in the web of Daniel, seeking to unanchor structure from meaning. It is thought that when a Prophet's rebirth is disrupted or their echo is silenced, Yar'galeth drinks the fragments. _"In the dark of the Prophet's Throat, he drank what Daniel forgot to weave."_
