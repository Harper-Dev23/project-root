# Daniel – The Silent Spinner

*Major Prophet of hidden structure, fate-webs, and subterranean prophecy*

## Domain & Aspect

Daniel is the Major Prophet of hidden structure, future-weaving, and unseen truth. As the Silent Spinner, he is the master of interconnected fates — divine webs that stretch beneath all things. His silence is not passive, but meditative; each quiet moment is a thread pulled toward meaning.

## Manifestation & Rebirth

Daniel manifests as a massive, otherworldly spider of obsidian chitin and golden-thread eyes, his body a cathedral of silk and whispering prophecy. He is best perceived in the shadows between truths — within cave-laced ruins, under forgotten temples, or in the tunnels beneath prophecy itself.

When slain or unbound, he always returns as a hidden beast of wide influence: sand serpents that map the dunes, trench-dwelling titans, or burrowed eyes beneath Behel'ith. Rebirth occurs after great truths are broken, reforming beneath Lake Genesis.

## Behavior & Influence

Daniel rarely intervenes directly — instead he arranges, plots, and binds. He appears to those uncovering deep lore, solving riddles, or nearing the collapse of fate, and protects what lies beneath, spiritually and literally.

His wrath is precision and entrapment: entire cities may wake surrounded by unbreakable thread, heretics become wrapped in fate and live only long enough to deliver their prophecy, and those who deceive sacred truth are rewritten into his web and remembered wrong.

## Followers & Taboos

His cult, **The Builders Beneath**, operates in silence, deep places, and under cities — tending vast networks of tunnels, dream-routes, and memory-threaded vaults, and keeping the sacred diagrams that map future forks. They are not loud, but everything they do matters later.

Breaking a knot of fate, interrupting sacred diagrams, or exposing hidden truths without offering protection are grave offenses. Those who offend Daniel may find their names erased from cause — their actions forgotten, their effects nullified.

## Lore & Myth

It is said Daniel once spun the first structure of Behel'ith — a web that held time steady — and that he whispered the other Prophets into form before they knew themselves. Some believe he was once one of the Kings, cast down after refusing to lead openly; others claim he is the only Prophet to have never died, only folded inward until forgotten. Daniel does not speak often, but when he does, his words reroute destiny.
