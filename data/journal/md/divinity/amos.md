# Amos – The Wrath of the Just

*Minor Prophet of divine wrath, judgment, and unstoppable justice*

## Domain & Aspect

Amos is the Minor Prophet of retributive force. As the Wrath of the Just, he represents divine strength unleashed when injustice becomes unbearable.

## Manifestation & Behavior

He takes the form of a towering bear-beast with sigil-marked hide and breath like an ice wind, and upon rebirth remains a massive beast of judgment — bear, frost lion, or storm bull. His coming is marked by crushed forests, stone cleaved by claw, and an unnatural hush before divine fury.

Amos does not roar unless the sentence is death. He is feared by many, even among divine agents, for his inflexible purity of wrath. He walks in silence — judgment is the only warning.

## Known Signs

Drawn to the Frozen Steppes, where cold mirrors his clarity and storms bear his stillness.
