# Ezekiel – The Patterned Visionary

*Major Prophet of divine architecture, recursion, and celestial order*

## Domain & Aspect

Ezekiel is the Prophet of sacred symmetry and recursive design, embodying the divine architecture that binds form and meaning across reality. He does not speak directly but reveals prophecy through dreams, mirrored structures, and sacred math. His presence coincides with spiritual reordering — or unraveling.

## Manifestation & Rebirth

Ezekiel most often appears as a vast Coatl — a winged serpent coiled in sigils and mirrored starlight. He is most clearly glimpsed in reflections, ruins, or dreams; his presence distorts geometry, induces déjà vu, or loops visions.

When slain or disrupted, he dissolves into mirrored stardust. After thirty-three nights of silence, he reforms in Lake Genesis, taking on a new recursive form — sometimes fractal-winged, sometimes serpentine, always recursive.

## Behavior & Influence

Ezekiel appears during ritual imbalance or when truth is buried too long, subtly altering terrain — tilting staircases, echoing steps, multiplying thresholds. His visions repeat or mutate until they are understood.

When offended, he releases the Dreamborn Beasts — hybrid monstrosities of sigils, fragments, and divine noise that hunt down heretics, false prophets, and disruptors of order, dissolving into divine ink upon defeat.

## Followers & Taboos

His followers, the **Architects of Order**, design temples with impossible angles, record visions in recursive glyphwork, and walk mirrored halls to learn patience. Jonah, Joel, and Obadiah all serve as aspects within his domain.

Speaking prophecy backward, breaking sacred circles, or thinking linearly in consecrated space are taboo. Those who defile his sigils often vanish from all memory — rewritten, or removed.

## Lore & Myth

It is said Ezekiel was the first beast to understand silence, and thus built the shapes that made other words possible — some call him the scribe of the stars, encoding divine order itself. He is one of the few Prophets believed to have rejected both Kings, considering their battle a flawed symmetry. Some sects claim he once loved a False God and wrote her name into the first loop, only to erase it when her worship failed; others whisper he has already returned from his final death, and all current visions are echoes of a beast long gone.
