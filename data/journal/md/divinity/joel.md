# Joel – The Swarm Caller

*Minor Prophet of apocalypse rhythm, divine balance, and coordinated judgment*

## Domain & Aspect

Joel is the Minor Prophet of divine balance and swarm-born consequence. He embodies apocalyptic rhythm — the beat of inevitability that arrives when the scales are tipped too far. His presence heralds reckoning through movement, pattern, and sound.

## Manifestation & Rebirth

Joel is always a swarm-creature, most commonly a dense locust cloud, wasp mass, or unified colony of radiant beetles — his body is many, but his will is one. He is felt through synchronized sound: insect clicking, rhythmic buzzing, rising hums. He reforms wherever natural systems collapse unjustly — failed harvests, poisoned springs, sites of hoarded power — manifesting first as a shadow, then consuming, then vanishing.

## Behavior & Influence

He moves with perfect coordination, balancing ecosystems through destruction — devouring excess, interrupting greed, leaving no waste and no message, only a rebalanced void.

When provoked, clouds of stinging insects descend in fractal shapes, rhythmic chirps grow into deafening waves, and entire villages disappear under synchronized pulses of wing and will. Joel's judgment is not personal — it is patterned correction.

## Followers & Taboos

His rare adherents include the **Chorus-Keepers**, monks who chant in pulses to attune with divine balance, the **Cicadites**, cloaked wanderers who travel in seven-year cycles spreading swarm wisdom, and the **Threshers**, masked priests who appear during famine or judgment festivals. He is fiercely loyal to Ezekiel, seen as his balancing edge.

Hoarding food beyond necessity, breaking rhythmic prayer, or killing swarms out of fear are taboo. Those who offend Joel are visited not by wrath, but by removal.

## Lore & Myth

It is said Joel was once Ezekiel's voice, shattered into countless bodies so balance could speak louder than words. Some claim every swarm has a seed-core, a divine particle, and those who find it are shown a pattern of the end of days. Some heresies believe Joel was created by the first act of imbalance, not born from divinity but from excess; others say he once judged a Prophet, and Ezekiel embraced him instead of rejecting him.
