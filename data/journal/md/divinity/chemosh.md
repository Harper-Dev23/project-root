# Chemosh – The Thunder Maw

*Anomalous False God of divine rage, thunder without justice, and the broken echo of judgment*

## Domain & Distortion

Chemosh is the echo of judgment made beast — a terrible mimicry of Micah, the Mammoth of Mercy. Where Micah stands firm, Chemosh storms through, obliterating all distinction between sin and soil. _"The sky breaks not in justice, but in appetite."_

He warps justice into violence, replacing discernment with destruction — a god of verdicts delivered without trial. Those wronged seek him out to strike without mercy, but the price is always more than the target.

## Manifestation & Influence

He takes the form of a lightning-wreathed Tyrannosaur whose roar precedes earthquakes. His signs include tremors before storms, storm-choked dreams, and bones fused by divine heat. His influence brings destruction of sacred structures, beasts corrupted by his roar, and storm-possessed cultists calling for "Final Reckonings."

## Known Heresies & Cults

The **Heresy of the Skyfire Scale** declares "let the heavens weigh nothing." His cult, **The Thunder-Marked**, provokes judgment from the skies, incites storms, and manipulates divine anger as a weapon.

## Prophet Interference

_"They showed him Micah's tusks, and Chemosh howled — not in fear, but in rage."_ Though he once mirrored Micah, Chemosh turned from judgment to fury. Micah's warmth unmoors him, cracking the false mirror of his form.
