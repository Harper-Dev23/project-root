# The Divine Structure of Behel'ith

## The Prophets

The Prophets are divine beasts — non-humanoid entities who serve as avatars of metaphysical truth. They are not worshipped in the traditional sense but **interpreted**, feared, or followed.

There are two tiers of Prophets:

- **Major Prophets** — Each governs a vast domain (e.g., recursion, silence, decay).
- **Minor Prophets** — Three are linked to each Major Prophet. They act as aspects, expressions, or conflicting truths within their master's sphere.

Prophets never appear in humanoid form. Their manifestations are always **beastial**, **abstract**, or **natural phenomena**.

## Rebirth & Transformation

When slain, unbound, or erased, a Prophet dissolves into divine essence. After a period of **ritual silence**, they are reborn within Lake Genesis.

- Their form changes with each rebirth, though symbolic continuity remains.
- Prophets may return as fractal serpents, rune-burdened lions, trees of memory, or unknowable hybrids.

Their true nature is recursive: every rebirth deepens their mystery, never resolves it.

## The False Gods

False Gods are divine anomalies that **oppose the cyclical order** of the Prophets. Some were born from corrupted prophecy, others may be twisted remnants of ancient truths.

- Often seductive, embodying power without cost.
- Worship of False Gods grants rapid boons — but leads to decay, madness, or paradox.
- Some False Gods whisper through Prophet dreams, attempting to unwrite them.

Unlike the Prophets, False Gods are not reborn — they **persist**, hidden in memory, dream, or ruin. The Prophets ask: what do you witness? The False Gods whisper: what do you deserve?

## Lake Genesis

Lake Genesis is the **divine womb** of Behel'ith — the source and cradle of rebirth for all Prophets.

- It mirrors the sky even at night.
- Time coils strangely near its shores.
- It is forbidden to walk upon without invitation or sign.

Prophets return here in symbolic death, reforming when prophecy demands. The lake is guarded not by beasts, but by silence itself.

## Divine Relationships & Worship

Prophets do not form pantheons, but **tension-chains**: recursive linkages of meaning, conflict, and purpose. Major Prophets often oppose or fold into the work of others, and Minor Prophets may betray their master in vision, though never in flesh. Some speculate there was once a Prophet of Unity — long erased — whose absence allows conflict to persist.

Prophets are followed by interpreters, beast-priests, and dream-scribes. No Prophet demands worship, but each reshapes the world through interpretation. To know a Prophet is not to serve — it is to change.

## Known Truths

- Prophets always return, though never the same.
- False Gods lie — but often from within sacred dreams.
- Lake Genesis is the only place where divine contradiction is silent.
- All prophecy is true. The question is when.
