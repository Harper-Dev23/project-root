# Zephaniah – The Herald of Light

*Minor Prophet of prophetic foresight, daylight omens, and divine warning*

## Domain & Aspect

Zephaniah is the Minor Prophet of foresight, divine warning, and radiant clarity. Known as the Herald of Light, he exists to precede judgment — a winged sign that what comes next is not avoidable, only witnessed. His light does not soothe, it reveals.

## Manifestation & Rebirth

He currently manifests as a brilliant wyvern with golden-white scales and thunder in his cry, and when slain reincarnates in a new avian form — never nocturnal, never of the night. He is seen in the open sky on clear days, his silhouette often marking the beginning of divine unraveling.

## Behavior & Influence

Zephaniah flies above sacred zones days before divine events, speaking in sky-patterns, sun glares, and atmospheric shifts. He refuses all darkness, never entering shadowed ruins, cursed valleys, or eclipsed lands.

He does not smite — he unveils. He casts great shadows ahead of calamity, causes sunlight to fixate unnaturally on the judged, and breathes a screech that shakes illusions loose. He is the moment prophecy becomes undeniable.

## Followers & Taboos

Rarely worshipped alone, his reverence is tied to Jeremiah's broader cult. Omens tied to his form include halos around the sun, feathers found in unusual places, and sky-dreams full of falling light — he is regarded by some as a sacred alarm, blessed but never prayed to.

Denying visible truths, cloaking prophecy in shadows, or manipulating foreknowledge for mortal gain are taboo. Those who break his tenets may find themselves blinded to divine warning, or lit up for others to see.

## Lore & Myth

It is said Zephaniah was once a divine bell whose tone became wings — he rang not to summon, but to warn. Legends claim he once marked a False God before it fell, and the sky turned gold with satisfaction. Some scholars debate whether he chooses his form or if the light itself binds him to it; others claim he is Jeremiah's first creation, born not from grief but from clarity. He is not salvation — he is announcement.
