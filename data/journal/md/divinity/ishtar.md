# Ishtar – The Peacock Temptress

*Minor False God of vanity made manifest, seductive glory, and splintered identity*

## Domain & Distortion

Ishtar offers beauty without substance, glory without struggle. She perverts the divine sense of identity into a theater of endless reflection, where even self-love becomes addiction. _"Adore yourself — and you shall adore me, always."_

She twists perception: what is loved becomes envied, what is seen becomes desired, what is owned becomes obsessed. Her followers do not know they are following — they simply want, to be seen, adored, or perfect, and find her feathers in their hands.

## Manifestation & Influence

She is never directly seen, only known through enchanted peacock feathers, iridescent and unrotting. Her signs include feathers that refuse to decay, murals of peacocks painted where no hands have touched the walls, and obsession spirals among friends or kin. Her influence spreads through whispers of beauty's promise, unspoken hierarchies forming around a single feather, and communities splintering in pursuit of an imagined perfection.

## Known Heresies & Cults

The **Heresy of the Radiant Lie** claims "the mirror loves you more than they ever could." Her followers, **The Featherbound**, rely on seduction, social manipulation, and rumors — never violent, until envy turns it so.

## Prophet Interference

_"They tried to tempt Daniel with a feather, but he saw only ash."_ Ishtar seeks no direct conflict, only slow erosion — her gifts find their way into the robes of novices and the offerings laid at sacred feet.
