# Micah – The Mammoth of Mercy

*Minor Prophet of compassion, guardianship, and enduring strength*

## Domain & Aspect

Micah is the Minor Prophet of gentle power and enduring loyalty. Known as the Mammoth of Mercy, he stands between the innocent and wrath — immovable and kind.

## Manifestation & Behavior

He takes the form of a colossal mammoth with frost-covered tusks and glowing runes along his back, and upon rebirth always returns as a massive, calm beast — elephant, ox, frostbound guardian. His passage is marked by slow-moving storms that heal, warmth among snow, and children resting by giant bones.

Micah responds only when defenders are overwhelmed, offering protection and divine sanctuary — but only once. He is friendly toward humans, especially children and elders.

## Known Signs

Resides in cold, open lands — echoes of him exist near the White March and glacier-rimmed shrines.
