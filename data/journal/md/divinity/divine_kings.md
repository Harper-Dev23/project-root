# The Divine Kings of Behel'ith

The Divine Kings are ancient, godlike beings who shape Behel'ith not through rebirth or seduction, but through **perpetual influence** and opposition. Their forms are constant. Their battle is the world itself.

## King Solomon — The Wielder of Wisdom

- **Form:** A towering ape of bronze fur, obsidian claws, and luminous, geometric eyes.
- **Domain:** Wisdom, divine tools, structure, sovereign clarity.
- **Presence:** His passing leaves weapons, tools, or sanctified ruins behind — shaped with impossible precision.
- **Faith:** Builders, tacticians, archivists, and militant monks honor him.

Solomon represents **order earned** — the faith of the designed and deliberate.

## King David — The True Vine

- **Form:** Ever-growing thorns, roots, and crimson-veined vines — sentient and slow.
- **Domain:** Faith, wild growth, organic resistance, inevitability.
- **Presence:** His influence can be found in overgrowth that overtakes memory, altars overgrown in hours, and roots that pulse beneath sacred stone.
- **Faith:** Mystics, druids, silent prophets, and blind pilgrims follow his weave.

David represents **faith without seeing** — belief as bloom and thorn.

## Divine Opposition

Though both are divine, Solomon and David are eternally opposed:

- **Embodiment** — Solomon: mind, design, clarity. David: growth, trust, instinct.
- **Tools** — Solomon: crafted divine weapons. David: natural overgrowth, entanglement.
- **Faith** — Solomon: structured hierarchy. David: wild devotion.
- **Color** — Solomon: gold and obsidian. David: crimson and vinegreen.

Sacred places across Behel'ith often bear both marks — thorny vines threading through collapsed towers, or a sculpted crown buried beneath living bark.

Some heresies suggest the Kings were once **one being**, split at the root of prophecy. Others say they predate even the Prophets, and that the entire island is their quarrel given shape.
