# Dagon – The River Lurker

*Major False God of submerged hunger and fear beneath the surface*

## Domain & Distortion

Dagon embodies hunger masquerading as providence. Where a Prophet brings enlightenment through ritual, Dagon brings only the illusion of safety in surrender — the lie that stillness brings peace, when it only invites consumption. _"What waits below waits forever. And it is always hungry."_

He distorts the divine idea of listening — he does not answer prayer, he consumes it, replacing reflection with dread. His followers are offered protection from storm and famine, if they sacrifice. But the river never stays sated.

## Manifestation & Influence

He takes the form of a titanic alligator god whose scaled hide bears scars shaped like sunken temples. His signs include scaled footprints that vanish upstream, bloated fish floating belly-up, and the sudden stilling of swamp sounds. He is found in swamp altars made of fishbone and mud, in visions within flooded dreams, and in ritual drownings misnamed "baptisms."

## Known Heresies & Cults

The **Heresy of the Hollow Current** teaches that "the river asks for your breath." His cult, the **Gilled Pilgrims**, thrives through rural isolation, inherited rituals, swampbound initiations, and generational possession through blood rites.

## Prophet Interference

Dagon cannot withstand the presence of certain deep-listening forces, and waters touched by such memory — like Lake Ezra or the River Wormwood — are poison to him. Where true listening occurs, Dagon flees.
