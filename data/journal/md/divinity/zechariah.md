# Zechariah – The Riddle Sentinel

*Minor Prophet of prophetic riddles, divine challenge, and truth-locked passage*

## Domain & Aspect

Zechariah is the Minor Prophet of riddles, challenge, and locked truth. As the Riddle Sentinel, he guards sacred doors — not with violence, but with questions that burn the soul.

## Manifestation & Behavior

He takes the form of a sphinx-beast with leonine grace and eyes that mirror starlight, and upon rebirth always takes the form of a regal guardian — guardian jackal, celestial lion, divine hawk-hybrid. His presence is marked by carved riddles in holy ruins, echoing whispers in vaults, and doorways that won't open without an answer.

He does not punish, but those who guess wrong often disappear. He leaves clues in old languages and visual puzzles, and sometimes waits centuries for a worthy question.

## Lore & Trials

It is said Zechariah once blocked a False God from claiming a ruined temple by asking it a question it had never considered — he protects not the door, but the meaning behind it.

Scattered across Behel'ith, his puzzle shrines manifest in ruins, altars, and micro-zones as spiritual tests of logic, memory, and divine clarity — not barriers, but invitations to deeper truth. Each puzzle represents a tier of prophetic insight, increasing in complexity and sacred consequence, and success often grants knowledge of hidden paths, access to divine vaults, or communion with his riddled vision. To face a puzzle shrine is to step into his gaze, and be measured by your willingness to question and understand.
