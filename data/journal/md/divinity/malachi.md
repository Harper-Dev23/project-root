# Malachi – The Burrower

*Minor Prophet of subterranean pathways, hidden truths, and unseen movement*

## Domain & Aspect

Malachi is the Minor Prophet of hidden paths and earth-bound prophecy. Known as the Burrower, he shapes and protects what lies beneath — not to hide it, but to prepare it. His domain is unseen movement, buried truth, and sacred disorientation.

## Manifestation & Rebirth

He takes the form of a colossal bulette clad in cracked armor etched with slow-burning runes, and always returns as a burrowing beast — mole-dragon, sand serpent, obsidian-clawed pangolin. Most never see him; his presence is known through tremors, sinkholes, and holy passages torn open without warning.

## Role & Behavior

Malachi tunnels beneath sites of spiritual density — the Verdant Shroud, the Valley of Joshua, the Ecclesian Forest — leaving paths that may be shortcuts between shrines, labyrinths for divine trial, or traps that swallow false pilgrims. He appears during seismic divine events, or when forbidden relics are disturbed.

He does not speak — his tunnels are his language, often mistaken for disaster until prophecy aligns his intent. He burrows in loops, spirals, or recursive glyph-patterns.

He does not chase, but the land collapses in his wake: offenders may fall through old vaults or sacred dead-zones, and survivors may emerge changed, as if reshaped by prophecy. Warnings include rhythmic tremors, sand shaped like sigils, or hymns sung through cracks.

## Followers & Taboos

He is worshipped through underground altars, collapsed sanctuaries, and rune-etched pit walls by a small, secretive, nomadic cult whose believers walk barefoot over sacred fault lines as tribute.

Building false altars atop his tunnels, sealing sacred caverns with greed or fear, or speaking his name underground without purpose are taboo. Those who break taboo may find their paths inverted, walking in circles, returning to griefs thought left behind.

## Lore & Myth

It is said Malachi once tunneled beneath the resting place of a Major Prophet, not to steal but to reroute vengeance away from innocent cities. Some claim his tunnels connect even to the underlayers of the False Gods' sanctums, where prophecy digs deeper than worship. He is the pulse beneath sacred silence.
