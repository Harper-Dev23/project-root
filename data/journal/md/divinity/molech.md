# Molech – The Hunger Below

*Major False God of devouring time, cauterized futures, and the hunger of potential*

## Domain & Distortion

Molech corrupts the divine principle of destiny into a devouring spiral, where possibility becomes fuel. He does not lie, nor tempt with falsehood — instead he burns paths unwalked, feeding on what could have been. _"He does not burn you. He burns your then."_

He consumes agency by removing choice. Worshipers find their options wither until only one remains, and that too is devoured. He grants clarity, removing fear of the unknown — in exchange, he takes the unknown itself.

## Manifestation & Influence

He takes the form of a colossal, fire-blooded salamander buried beneath the Springs of Sheol, lined in molten runes and armored scale. His signs include water that boils without fire, visions of futures erased, and scarless brands across the chest or jaw. Cultists speak only in absolutes, never "maybe" or "if"; relics radiate warmth but cast no light; sacrifices vanish entirely — not body, but their timeline.

## Known Heresies & Cults

The **Heresy of the One Path** holds that every action not taken is waste, and only the inevitable should remain. His cult, the **Furnace Keepers**, preaches spiritual certainty and offers visions of "your final self," enforcing fate by removing options.

## Prophet Interference

Molech clashes most violently with Obadiah, the Prophet of reversal and ambiguity. While Obadiah guards the unchosen, Molech seals the gate behind every step. _"Obadiah is the lock. Molech is the melted key."_
