# Hosea – The Gentle Horn

*Minor Prophet of blessing, mercy, and divine intervention*

## Domain & Aspect

Hosea is the Minor Prophet of unexpected mercy. Known as the Gentle Horn, he appears to bless mortals in moments of desperation, offering light to those of good heart.

## Manifestation & Behavior

He takes the form of a radiant unicorn — pure white, hooved, and glowing from within — and upon rebirth always returns as a holy, horned beast of healing and purity. His passage is marked by frost-kissed flowers, hoofprints surrounded by calm, and a sudden stillness in danger.

Hosea grants blessings only in moments of true need, and will not approach the corrupt or self-serving. He is most often seen at the edge of battles, or among the wounded faithful.

## Known Signs

Prefers cold climates where heat fails — his gentle light stands strongest in silence and snow.
