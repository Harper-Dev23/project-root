# Jonah – The Deep Mystery

*Minor Prophet of oceanic prophecy, evasion, and the fear of the unseen*

## Domain & Aspect

Jonah is the Minor Prophet of the unknown ocean, of submerged truths and fear cloaked in revelation. His domain stretches from drowning prophecy to silence beneath understanding. False Gods fear his depths, and mortals feel his pull like gravity.

## Manifestation & Rebirth

Always aquatic, his true shape is never fully seen — manifestations range from tentacled leviathans to whale-sized masses wrapped in seaweed and light. He is seen only in silhouette or reflection, speaking through shipwrecks, drowned dreams, and cold tides. He reforms beneath Lake Genesis or along the trench of the Prophet's Throat, his return heralded by unnatural stillness, backwards tides, and relics rising from the sea.

## Behavior & Influence

Jonah haunts coasts and deep waters, rarely emerging, appearing only to those near transformation, death, or unspeakable knowledge. Surrounding water silences — waves halt, birds vanish, and sound is stolen.

He does not attack — he pulls. He invokes panic in the minds of seafarers, collapses boats inward into themselves, and sends dreaming prophets spiraling into water-logic and recursive drowning. Even corrupted tides yield to him, and False Gods avoid his presence.

## Followers & Taboos

His cult is fragmentary: **Tidewalkers**, hermits who chant to him waist-deep in night surf; **Deepbearers**, who carry his relics or drowned visions; and **Dream-Vessels**, mortals whose minds are partially flooded with his voice. Though he serves Ezekiel, Jonah is never commanded, only understood.

Building ships without blessing the keel, speaking prophecy beneath stormlight, or looking into still waters at night without permission are taboo. Those who break these may vanish — not drowned, but rewritten into tides.

## Lore & Myth

It is said Jonah is older than language, that he listened to prophecy before it could be spoken. Some believe he carries part of a False God inside him, broken and chained; others say he is not a beast at all, but the memory of the first abyss made animate. Some whisper Jonah was never truly Minor — that he refused to ascend as a Major.
