# Haggai – The Tireless Architect

*Minor Prophet of reconstruction, divine architecture, and sacred labor*

## Domain & Aspect

Haggai is the Minor Prophet of sacred rebuilding. Known as the Tireless Architect, he restores what was once holy — shrines, sanctuaries, and ruins marked by prophecy.

## Manifestation & Behavior

He takes the form of an ant-beast of divine proportions, strong, precise, and tireless. Upon rebirth he always returns as a builder-creature — termite, mole-crab, or sacred burrower. His passage leaves red sand trails, pillar arrangements in forgotten places, and collapsed ruins slowly restoring themselves.

He works silently and cooperatively, never speaking, and is seen at dawn when light strikes cracked marble or forgotten glyphs. He respects the ruins of False Gods, but rebuilds only that which was divinely true.

## Lore & Myth

It is said Haggai once rebuilt an entire shrine overnight, using the bones of its defenders as structural memory. He does not dream. He builds.
