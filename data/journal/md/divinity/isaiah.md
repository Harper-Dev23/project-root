# Isaiah – The Burning Branch

*Major Prophet of divine justice, faith, and valor*

## Domain & Aspect

Isaiah is the Major Prophet of divine justice, faith, and courageous conviction. Known as the Burning Branch, he is both flame and fang — a radiant beast of righteous wrath and protective clarity. Where corruption festers, Isaiah arrives as both judgment and shield.

## Manifestation & Rebirth

Isaiah most often appears as a lion wreathed in flame, eyes like molten gold — a form that always exudes power, nobility, and radiance. When not beastial, he may manifest as a burning branch, a symbol that speaks to the worthy. He often arrives during crisis or injustice, seen as fire on the horizon or in dreams filled with roaring warmth.

When slain, his flame flickers across the sky and is rekindled in a new beast of valor — always a proud predator, never lowly, never broken. He reforms within Lake Genesis during moments of collective mortal courage.

## Behavior & Influence

Isaiah intervenes when justice is defied too long, walking in the hearts of warriors, dreamers, and defenders. His passing often leaves the scent of myrrh and ash, and his pawprints can burn stone.

His wrath is flame and roar: pillars of fire erupt when oaths are broken, his roar shatters false idols and sanctifies battlefields, and his gaze alone can purge a corrupted soul — or set it ablaze.

## Followers & Taboos

His faithful, **The Flameward Host**, are composed of radiant beasts, phoenix-riders, and torchbearing seers who act as judges, protectors, and frontline defenders of divine law, often intervening in mortal conflict when justice falters. They are feared and honored, for their mercy is as absolute as their judgment.

Betraying oaths sworn in light, cowardice before known evil, or allowing injustice when action is possible are taboo. Offenders may feel Isaiah's presence in burning visions — or be hunted by radiant beasts.

## Lore & Myth

It is said Isaiah once guarded a gate to a higher realm and chose to stay behind to defend the innocent — the flame that chose to burn low so others could find light. Some legends say he weeps flame when forced to punish the faithful, but will not stop. Some sects claim he was the first Prophet to voluntarily fall, choosing action over divine detachment; others whisper his burning-branch form is a shard of something greater, a divine covenant forgotten even by the Kings.
