# Tammuz – The False King

*Major False God of rotten order, the crown in decay, and faith hollowed by power*

## Domain & Distortion

Tammuz wears the mask of King David, mimicking divine kingship with poisoned purpose. His rule is not one of unity, but control through tradition, growth turned rancid, law crusted over with rot. _"Why rise, when the crown already rests?"_

He twists faith into obedience, heritage into stagnation, ritual into bureaucracy — his kingship is unchallenged because his followers are too withered to speak. He appears as the rightful heir, offering stability, legacy, and peace, but only under total subservience.

## Manifestation & Influence

He takes the form of a moss-draped monarch formed of stone and vine, his face always veiled by a golden mask. His signs include statues that bloom with black orchids, psalms heard where no mouths move, and thrones forming from trees overnight. His influence breeds root-cloister cults in overgrown cities, undead kingships, and faded banners still saluted by no armies.

## Known Heresies & Cults

The **Heresy of the Golden Mask** insists "the King was never dead." His cult, the **Crownroot Faithful**, exploits nostalgia, revives corrupted royal lines, and subverts order through ceremony and inheritance rites.

## Prophet Interference

_"They carved his crown from David's dreams. But it did not fit."_ Tammuz cannot unseat King David, but attempts to haunt his wake — his presence is strongest in regions where David has not passed, or where memory of rightful kingship fades.
