# The White Veil – The False Sleep

*Major False God of stillness eternal, frozen memory, and dreams that never wake*

## Domain & Distortion

The White Veil is a blasphemous echo of Micah, the Mammoth of Mercy — turning the virtues of patience, endurance, and silence into stagnation, forgetting, and dreams that devour the self. She offers no madness, no hunger, only sleep. _"What if you rested here? What if nothing ever hurt again?"_

She distorts peace into paralysis — her stillness is not serenity but decay, a forgetting of fire, name, and purpose. Her victims seek rest, a moment of stillness; that moment becomes eternity.

## Manifestation & Influence

She appears as a towering figure cloaked in stitched fur and ice, sometimes glimpsed as a frostbitten beast or a still, shivering woman. Her signs include warmth vanishing without cause, voices trailing into silence, and fresh snow undisturbed where footsteps should be. Pilgrims are found frozen mid-prayer, beasts curled peacefully in death, and relics of memory turned inert.

## Known Heresies & Cults

The **Heresy of the Final Mercy** teaches that "to move no more is to suffer no more." Her cult, **The Stillborne**, practices gentle indoctrination, ritual hibernation, quiet suicides mistaken for transcendence, and snow-sigil tracing.

## Prophet Interference

_"She touched the edge of Micah's vision — and recoiled from the warmth."_ The White Veil dreads Micah; his tremors, his carvings, and even spoken stories of his courage unmake her influence. She cannot remain near acts of memory, resistance, or judgment.
