# Baal – The Black Flame Bull

*Major False God of divine inversion, wrath made flesh, and corruption of law*

## Domain & Distortion

Baal is inversion itself — the sacred turned inside out, the judgment of gods refashioned as fury without balance. He mocks the Ceremony, the Hunt, and the Law, branding those who seek order with chaos. _"Let the sacred burn. Let the witness scream."_

His corruptive nature turns devotion into combustion — his influence warps sacred rites into infernos of self-destruction. He tempts with power without constraint, justice as flame; his followers believe the world must be purged to be pure.

## Manifestation & Influence

He appears as a colossal bull wreathed in eternal fire, molten eyes leaking judgment — in visions, his breath incinerates names. His passing leaves ashes where prayer was spoken, brands appearing unbidden on flesh, and sacred glyphs burned backward. His influence breeds fire-maddened cults, sacrificial pyres posed as "cleansing rites," and ruins that cannot be unburnt.

## Known Heresies & Cults

The **Heresy of the Burning Word** preaches "let all law be ash." His cult, the **Flamebrand Covenant**, co-opts religious structures through zealotry, ceremonial arson, and mass visions of purifying destruction.

## Prophet Interference

_"Isaiah cried out, and Baal answered in fire. But truth stood unburning."_ Baal scars even the memory of Prophets — few survive direct encounter. Daniel is said to bear the echo of Baal's flame within his left eye, a burden he will never forgive.
