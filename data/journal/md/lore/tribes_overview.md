# The Four Tribes

**Elseth — The Tribe of Coin and Counsel**  
Values wealth, diplomacy, knowledge. Regal attire and pacts.

**Styx — The Tribe of Beasts and Bone**  
Values simplicity, beast‑communion, memory, instinct. Furs, carved bone totems, spirit listening.

**Zafaar — The Tribe of Ash and Fang**  
Values strength, fury, honor. Charred armor, fire‑blood tattoos; the bold and unbroken.

**Le’sse — The Tribe of Bloom and Veil**  
Values grace, artistry, sacred growth. Bark‑woven garments, flower sigils; ritual enchanters.
