# Peoples of the World

Behel'ith is an island, and a small one. Everything that walks it came from somewhere else. What follows is what the tribes know of the wider world's peoples -- including several who have never set foot on the island.

For what a race means to a Hunter in play, see [[Playable Races]].

## Humans -- the Hunters

Native to the bottom-left continent, and the most numerous people alive. Warring nations, tribal in organisation and conquerors by habit; the small continent to the east has been hunted by them for generations. Four kingdoms of note, which is not a coincidence -- the four tribes on Behel'ith are their champions.

## Dwarves -- the Builders

A mountain people, at home in high stone and the clay and dirt beneath it. Their craft is graded by material: the harder the stone, the greater the prestige, and the rarer the better. Clay and salt sit at the bottom of that ladder.

## Elves -- the Thinkers

Of the northern island and the northern of the twin continents. Social standing and intelligence are coveted above nearly everything, and their people are more varied than outsiders assume -- **Shade**, **Coral**, **Dry**, **Wood** and **Brook** elves each keep their own customs.

The names are worn down from the places they came from. The Shade elves are from **Sha'de**; the Dry elves from **Dra'i**. Say either name to an elf in the old way and they will correct your vowels.

## Ferrow -- the Explorers

Nomadic, and drawn to high places -- their sky city is the one thing every account of them agrees on. Bright feathers in enormously varied patterns, and a people split between day and night hunters: hawkish and peacock-bright on one side, carrion and owl and bat and raven on the other.

## Wylett -- the Survivalists

Found throughout the thriving parts of the world rather than concentrated anywhere. Both prey and predator run in them -- rabbit, rodent and fishlike lines alongside fox, hyena, lion and bear.

## Skith -- the Deceptionists

Desert, jungle and sea. Cutthroats and pragmatists, with little patience for the distinction.

## Gnomes -- the Engineers

Secluded in jungle, mysterious, rare, and further ahead technologically than anyone who has not met them believes.

## Dragonborn -- the Worshippers

Of the north-western continent, shaped by proximity to dragons and by the split between Bahamut and Tiamat. A few travel with Elseth, whose wealth was built on dealings with dragonkind.

## Half-orcs -- the Mercenaries

The north-western continent's south-west coast. Warriors for hire, and where that fails, pirates and outlaws. Some ride with Zafaar.
