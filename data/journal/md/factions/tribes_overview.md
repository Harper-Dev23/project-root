# The Four Tribes

Four hunting parties keep camp on Behel'ith. They are not allies. They are four nations that spent generations killing each other, and the only reason they are not doing so now is that the Hunt gives them somewhere else to put it.

## Why there is a Hunt at all

The tribes come from different parts of **Oake** -- properly Oake'Kaan, though almost nobody says the whole of it any more -- and the history between them is mostly war. Grievances were settled by armies, and settled badly, the cost measured in tens of thousands.

The covenant replaced that. Rather than field armies, each nation sends champions across the sea to compete on sacred ground for the largest game. It is a lesser conflict by design: controlled, witnessed, and survivable. The tension did not go anywhere. It was given a smaller container.

Nothing about the arrangement makes the tribes like one another. It only makes killing each other expensive.

## Elseth -- The Tribe of Silk and Ledger

**Homeland:** Elseth, in northern Oake.
**Values:** wealth, diplomacy, knowledge, binding pacts.
**Appearance:** dragonscale and gold-trimmed robes, scroll-wrapped staffs, draconic motifs.

Traders and oath-makers, and the wealthiest of the four. Their sacred commerce descends from ancient dealings with dragonkind, and it shows -- fine metals, finer weapons, and armour nobody else can afford to field. Mostly human, with a few dragonborn among them.

Wren, their Animancer, speaks for them at camp.

## Zafaar -- The Tribe of Ash and Fang

**Homeland:** Eastern Oake, beside the great desert.
**Values:** strength, fury, honour in conquest.
**Appearance:** tribal and barbaric -- charred armour, fire-blood tattoos, tusked helms.

Large, strong, savage fighters carrying the biggest weapons on the island. Mostly human, with dwarves and half-orcs among their number, and smaller shackled humans besides -- a fact the other three tribes have never stopped holding against them.

Their champion Gorrek waits in the [[The Combat Pit]] for anyone who thinks the tribe overstates itself.

## Styx -- The Tribe of Beasts and Bone

**Homeland:** the Isles of Styx.
**Values:** simplicity, beast-communion, memory, instinct.
**Appearance:** modest dress, furs, carved bone totems, beast tattoos.

Smaller in stature and more agile than the rest, fighting with lighter arms and at range, alongside beasts they have tamed rather than broken. Mostly human, with some Skith among them. Beast-talkers and spirit listeners -- humble, strange, and the closest of the four to the wild itself.

Cade, their Tactician, speaks for them.

## Le'sse -- The Tribe of Bloom and Veil

**Homeland:** the Kingdom of Le'sse.
**Values:** grace, artistry, sacred growth, subtle power.
**Appearance:** shining metal under fine white cloaks; many in pale white robes, with flower-sigil jewellery.

Of average build and uncommon poise. Enchanters and keepers of memory, and the most mixed of the four -- mostly human, with elves and Wylett among them, which is where the old claim of distant elven blood comes from.

The duellists Ember and Rime speak for them.

## Who hates whom

- **Zafaar and Styx** carry the worst of it. Zafaar raiders assaulted the island peoples in the past, and Styx has not forgotten which islands those were. This is the one pairing where the covenant is doing visible work.
- **Elseth and Zafaar** are the two largest and most successful tribes, and behave accordingly. Theirs is a rivalry rather than a wound -- each measuring itself against the other, in wealth on one side and in force on the other.
- **Le'sse** keeps its distance from all three, which the other three variously read as grace or as calculation.

## Dealing with them

Each tribe offers its own quests and its own stock, and each grants a **Tribe Ticket** -- traded for unique, culturally sacred items no other tribe will sell. What any of them will actually open to you depends on standing: see [[Tribe Favor and Quests]].

You will pledge to one of them at [[The Elders' Tower]]. The other three will notice.
