# The Four Tribes

**Elseth - The Tribe of Coin and Counsel**  
Values wealth, diplomacy, and knowledge. Regal attire and formal pacts define their presence.

**Styx - The Tribe of Beasts and Bone**  
Values instinct and memory. They favor furs, carved bone totems, and long vigils in the wild.

**Zafaar - The Tribe of Ash and Fang**  
Values strength and honor. Charred armor and fire-scored tattoos mark the bold and unbroken.

**Le'tsse - The Tribe of Bloom and Veil**  
Values grace and sacred growth. Woven bark garments and living sigils fuel their ritual craft.
