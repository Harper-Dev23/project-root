# Tribe Favor and Quests

Every tribe tracks what it thinks of you as a single number, and that number decides how you are treated. Your **home tribe** -- the one you pledge to at [[The Elders' Tower]] -- uses its own set of ranks and can be taken further than the other three.

## Ranks

| Score | Home tribe | Other tribes |
|---|---|---|
| below -50 | Exiled | Hostile |
| -50 to -21 | Disgraced | Threatening |
| -20 to -6 | Unwelcome | Unfriendly |
| -5 to +25 | **Initiate** | **Neutral** |
| +26 to +65 | Member | Cordial |
| +66 to +105 | Trusted | Friendly |
| +106 and up | Champion | *(unreachable)* |

Everyone starts in the middle band. The starting band is deliberately wide, so small early shifts do not move your rank.

A tribe that is **not** your own is capped at **+105** -- Friendly is as close as an outsider gets. Only a home tribe reaches Champion.

## What each rank means

At **Initiate** or **Neutral** you get standard access: the lodge is open, the vendor will trade, and prices are ordinary.

Rising past that, a home tribe begins discounting its stock and treating your wounds more seriously -- 10% off and better healing at Member, 20% off and exclusive stock at Trusted. Other tribes offer smaller discounts and more conversation.

Falling below it closes doors in order: first the trade, then the dialogue, then the lodge itself. At the bottom a home tribe **exiles** you outright -- vendor gone, stash locked.

## Earning favor

Completing a tribe's leader trial is the largest single swing available early, worth **40 points** on its own. Beyond that, favor comes from doing a tribe's work: finishing contracts and hunts under its banner, donating gear and supplies to its stores, and bringing back intel on its rivals.

It is lost the same way it is earned, in reverse -- trading heavily with a rival, raiding one of its hunting parties, or abandoning a contract once accepted.

## Tribe Tickets

Separate from favor, each tribe grants a **Tribe Ticket**, spent at that tribe's own vendor row for unique, culturally sacred items. Standing decides what is on the shelf; the Ticket is what buys it.
