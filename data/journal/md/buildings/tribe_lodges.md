# The Tribe Lodges

Each of the four tribes keeps a lodge at [[Camp Nehemiah]] — part embassy, part barracks, part shopfront. A Hunter can enter any of them regardless of allegiance, though what they are shown depends entirely on standing.

Inside each lodge a Hunter will find the tribe's leader, its own vendor stock, and whatever work it is currently willing to hand to an outsider.

## The Four

- **Elseth** — the Animancers. Spirit-work and the reshaping of damage itself. Wren speaks for them.
- **Styx** — the Tacticians. Planning, positioning and field intelligence. Cade speaks for them.
- **Le'sse** — the Duelists. Elemental mastery and the duel. Ember and Rime speak for them.
- **Zafaar** — the Champions. Raw martial strength and the proving ground.

## Standing

Every lodge tracks what it thinks of you separately. A tribe that likes you opens its stock, its contracts and its maps; one that does not will let you through the door and no further. Your home tribe goes further still. See [[Tribe Favor and Quests]].
