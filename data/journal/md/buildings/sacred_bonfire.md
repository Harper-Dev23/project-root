# The Sacred Bonfire

An eternal flame at the heart of [[Camp Nehemiah]], and the reason the camp works at all. Oaths, initiations and quiet rituals all gather around it. It is the one fire on Behel'ith where Hunters from rival tribes sit together without drawing blood.

New Hunters are raised here — a party is assembled at the bonfire before it is anything else. It is also where Hunters return between hunts, and where the ones who did not return are spoken about.

No tribe claims the bonfire. No god claims it either. It simply has not gone out.
