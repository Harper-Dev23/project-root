# Camp Vendors

Camp Nehemiah's trade runs through two separate market rows. **Vendor Row** sells for coin and is open to anyone — weapons, armor and tonics, plus two stalls that take Hunt Tickets instead: Greenhollow Satchel's Hunt Plans, and Bonepile's weapon gamble once unlocked. **Tribe Vendor Row** sells tribe-unique pendants for Tribe Tickets, and only to Hunters who've pledged to that vendor's own tribe.

See the Vendor Directory under People for who actually runs each stall.
