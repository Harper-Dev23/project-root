# Camp Vendors

Camp Nehemiah's trade runs through two separate market rows. **Vendor Row** sells for coin and is open to anyone — weapons, armor, potions, and the free Wayfinder's Cache Hunt Plans, plus Bonepile's weapon gamble once unlocked. **Tribe Vendor Row** sells tribe-unique pendants for Tribe Tickets, and only to Hunters who've pledged to that vendor's own tribe.

See the Vendor Directory under People for who actually runs each stall.
