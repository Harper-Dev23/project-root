# The Elders' Tower

The tallest structure at [[Camp Nehemiah]], and the one place in camp where the four tribes are answerable to something other than themselves. [[Elder Berethos Thorne]] keeps the Hunt's law here.

A Hunter comes to the Tower twice in their early career. The first time is to be told what the Hunt actually is, once they have survived enough of it to be worth telling. The second is to pledge — to name which tribe they will call home.

## Pledging

Allegiance is declared at the Tower and cannot be undone. Before the Elder will hear a pledge, a Hunter is expected to have visited all four lodges — [[The Four Tribes|Elseth, Styx, Le'sse and Zafaar]] — and heard what each has to say for itself. Walking in having met only one tribe is not a decision; it is an accident.

The pledge sets a home tribe for good. Relations with the other three continue, and can be improved or ruined independently — see [[Tribe Favor and Quests]] — but only one tribe is ever *yours*.
