# The Seers' Tent

A quiet tent at the edge of [[Camp Nehemiah]], kept by [[The Seers]] — blind elders who speak only when stirred, and who are not stirred often.

They are not consulted the way a vendor is consulted. A Hunter goes to the Seers when something has happened that camp has no explanation for, and leaves with either an answer or the strong impression that they asked badly.

What they say tends to concern the Prophets, the False Gods, and the things met on the island that fit neither description — [[Lamentation]] among them.
