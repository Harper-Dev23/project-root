# The Hunt Gate

The only sanctioned way out of [[Camp Nehemiah]] and onto the island proper. Hunters assemble here, choose where they are going, and pass through.

The Gate is a threshold in the strict sense: **once you leave through it, you cannot return until the hunt ends.** Whatever you failed to pack, you are without. Whatever you failed to settle at camp stays unsettled.

## Before You Go

A hunt is chosen at the Gate — a destination, and a plan for how to approach it. Supplies are spent from what you carry; there is no resupply short of what your tribe is willing to send after you, and tribes only send help to Hunters they value.

Two grounds are open to new Hunters: [[The Reeds of Gethsemane]] and the [[Bay of Solace]]. The rest of the island is charted but closed — the Hunt has to reach them first.
