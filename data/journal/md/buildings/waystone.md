# The Waystone

The Waystone Shard is a rare, soulbound relic every Hunter carries — a fragment of the island's sacred waystone network, attuned to its owner. Using it opens two tracked sections:

- **Prophet Favor** — the island's prophets are aware of those who hunt in their domain. Favor reflects standing with each prophet, shaped by the nature of your hunts and the choices you make. Favor tracking itself is still in development.
- **Hunt Progress** — a map of waystone network nodes across the island (Elder Grove at Camp Nehemiah is attuned from the start; others unlock as the island opens up). Most nodes are still locked pending later content.

Both sections currently show their structure but not full live tracking yet — this is one of the more actively-developing systems in the game.
