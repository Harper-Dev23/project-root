# Camp Infrastructure Overview

[[Camp Nehemiah]] is neutral ground, raised by all four tribes on the eastern reach of the [[The River Wormwood]], downstream from the [[The Ruins of Exodus]]. Everything a Hunter needs between hunts is here, and nothing here belongs to any one tribe.

## What Stands at Camp

- **[[The Sacred Bonfire]]** — the eternal flame at the camp's heart, where parties are raised and oaths are sworn.
- **[[The Waystone]]** — the monolith that tracks Prophet Favor and the slow opening of the island's hunt-node network. Every Hunter carries a shard of it.
- **[[The Combat Pit]]** — the sparring ground, and the six training exercises run there.
- **[[The Elders' Tower]]** — where the Hunt's law is kept, and where allegiance is pledged.
- **[[The Tribe Lodges]]** — one per tribe: Elseth, Styx, Le'sse and Zafaar.
- **[[The Seers' Tent]]** — the blind elders, consulted rarely and reluctantly.
- **[[The Hunt Gate]]** — the only way out, and a one-way door until the hunt ends.
- **[[Camp Vendors]]** — Vendor Row and the Tribe Vendor Row, trading in Hunt Tickets and Tribe Tickets.

## Getting Around

Most of camp is open from the start. The Combat Pit opens as soon as a party exists; the Elders' Tower matters once a Hunter has survived enough to be told what they are actually doing; the Hunt Gate matters last, and only once.
