# The Combat Pit

A sunken sparring ground at [[Camp Nehemiah]] where Hunters test themselves between hunts. Nothing that happens here counts against you — the Pit exists so that the things which *do* count happen somewhere you have already practiced.

Six training exercises are run here, each unlocked by clearing the one before it. Clearing an exercise for the first time pays out Hunt Tickets.

## The Six Exercises

### I — Basic Training
Six stationary dummies that do nothing but stand there. For learning targeting, positioning and the basic flow of a turn with no incoming pressure. **12 Hunt Tickets.**

### II — Basic Training II
Six reinforced dummies that shuffle around the field and dodge out of ground hazards. They deal no damage; the lesson is range, movement and the shape of an area attack. **12 Hunt Tickets.**

### III — The Animated Six
The same six dummies, animated into distinct roles and geared up: Chad tanks and can redirect a blow onto himself, Stan channels real healing across the group, Gary and Lenny fight as casters with layered tactics, and Doug and Mo react the instant an ally is struck. The first exercise that fights back with intent. **12 Hunt Tickets.**

### IV — Huntsman & Beasts
A Styx huntsman commander directing two beasts — Oskar, who tears, and Kiro, who spits venom. Teaches you to read a pack: marked targets, coordinated bursts, and buffs that move through the whole group. **18 Hunt Tickets.**

### V — Elemental Duelists
Ember and Rime of the Le'sse, fighting as a pair. Fire and frost played against each other deliberately, punishing anyone who prepares for only one of them. **24 Hunt Tickets.**

### VI — Gorrek
A Zafaar berserker who punishes hesitation and tears open his own wounds for power. The hardest thing in the Pit. **30 Hunt Tickets.**

## Gorrek's Reckoning

Gorrek does not take losing well. Once he has been put down, he will meet a Hunter again — and again — each time harder to finish than the last.

Five Reckoning tiers follow the first victory, unlocked in order. He gains health, resilience and resistance far faster than he gains damage; the fight becomes longer rather than sharper. From the second tier he learns to clear his own head, and Disorient alone will no longer hold him. At the fourth, the air around him drags at a Hunter's initiative.

Tickets scale with the tier: **35, 40, 50, 60, and 75** for Reckoning I through V.
