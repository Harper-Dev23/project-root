# Shrines and Prophet Favor

Shrines stand at sacred sites across Behel'ith, and **Prophet Favor** is the standing a Hunter builds with the island's Prophets by attending to them.

The [[The Waystone]] at [[Camp Nehemiah]] is the anchor for it. Every Hunter carries a soulbound shard of it, and the shard is what tracks favor as it accrues.

## Where it stands

Neither shrines nor Prophet Favor are implemented yet. The Waystone tracks Hunt Points today; the favor half of what it is meant to measure does not exist in the field, and no shrine can currently be visited or attended.

What is settled is the shape: favor is earned per Prophet rather than as one pooled number, and the Prophets are not interchangeable -- see [[The Divine Structure of Behel'ith]] and the individual Prophet entries under Divinity.
