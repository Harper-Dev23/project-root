# Temptation (The Coral Lure)

Just offshore from Camp Nehemiah lies a maze-like coral reef where sunlight refracts strangely and voices call from beneath still water. Coral spires called the Reaching Coral Spires are shaped like outstretched hands, often mistaken from a distance for drowning figures, and illusory mirage zones lure swimmers further out than they mean to go. Beneath the calm surface, dangerous undercurrents run swift and deadly.

The reef is said to be touched by Asherah, or perhaps by the curse of a drowned prophet — Echo-Sirens whisper through the water, mimicking the voices of those long lost, and one old story claims the reef once silenced Augustine's own voice. Local legend remembers it as the place where people once tried to walk to heaven across the corals — and says the reef has slept, or prayed, ever since.
