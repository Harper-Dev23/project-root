# The Valley of Joshua

Bounded by the Mountains of Psalms to the northeast and the Mountains of Proverbs to the southwest, the Valley of Joshua is scorched plains strewn with shattered altars and Trial Spires glowing with rival sigils, thunder-cracked pillars looming over it all as monuments to legacy and conflict. The Challenger's Pit, a crater scarred by celestial duels, sits near the Bridge of Oaths, ruins said to mark where Joshua's betrayal unfolded; spectral warzones known as the Echo Battlegrounds are said to activate under starlight.

Champions of false gods walk this valley alongside divine manifestations of judgment and war-touched beasts drawn to any test of strength. The land bears, by all accounts, the legacy of conquest and betrayal — strength and loyalty are constantly tested here by divine echo. Local belief holds that Joshua's spirit may still stride unseen across the valley, guiding or judging whoever rises to take his place.
