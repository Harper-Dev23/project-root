# Mount Moriah

Set within the Mountains of Proverbs, Mount Moriah is a desolate peak that tends to appear in visions during moments of deep doubt or decision. The wind carries voices of former oaths across its slopes, and the soil is said to bleed faintly red.

Moriah is remembered as a place where one's will is weighed against divine demand — a moment of blade, where time seems to halt as fate hangs in the balance. It is held as a kind of covenant space, echoing with the potential of faith either rewarded or denied. Those tested here are said to glimpse alternate paths they didn't take, and certain divine relics are said to activate only on this mount, or in the hands of someone who has walked it spiritually. As the saying goes: it is where the blade pauses, and where heaven holds its breath.
