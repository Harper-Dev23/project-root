# The Wicked Coast

Along the west-northwest shoreline, the Wicked Coast is a stretch of jagged cliffs scourged by constant gales. Salt spray mixes with fine sand to form razor-edged gusts, and wrecked ships lie impaled on the reef known as Sinner's Teeth. Petrified ship masts called the Grief-Masts jut from the cliffs like macabre flags, a colony of salt-blind gulls screams unheeded warnings out over the water, and sea caves called the Weeping Grottos let brine fall in patterns that look almost like faces.

Phantom mariners bound to drowned vows haunt the wrecks, tide-cultists scavenge what the sea gives up, and crabs armored in barnacles grow as large as men along the reef. The winds here are said to be the breath of a forsaken sea-god, leading ships deliberately to ruin — prayers shouted into the gale are said to return only as mocking laughter. Local legend holds that once a century, the wind reverses for a single night, giving the coast's bound spirits a chance to escape their curse — though few ever do.
