# Mount Ararat

Rising in the Mountains of Proverbs, Mount Ararat is a jagged monolith that pierces both sky and myth — the final peak before the sea. Its face is fractured by ancient lightning strikes, and its base veils the entrance to Prophet's Throat below. No map of the island accurately places it; its ascent, by most accounts, is completed more often in visions than in footfall.

Ararat marks the boundary between divine vision and its unraveling — to climb it is said to open the inner ear to a forbidden clarity. While many seek its summit, the true revelation is said to lie not at the top but in the descent into Prophet's Throat beneath it. Once thought a place of alignment with prophetic truth, it is now held to guard the echoing heresy of Yar'galeth. Dreams near Ararat are said to repeat in recursive cycles, each iteration drawing closer to either truth or madness, and relics unearthed here are said to function only during lunar eclipses. As the local saying goes: Ararat does not pierce the sky, it splinters it — and the real fall begins only once you reach the summit.
