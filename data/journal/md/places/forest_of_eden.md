# Forest of Eden

At the island's center, surrounding the still waters of Lake Ezra, the Forest of Eden rises in thick, ancient trunks. Sunlight falls through the canopy in radiant columns, and the air carries a low, distant choir no one has ever found the source of. The forest is said to reshape its own paths according to the traveler's intent — those who enter seeking something rarely leave by the way they came.

Root circles hidden in the soil are said to lead down into the Chambers of Thought, and singing moss stones along the deeper trails hum with a resonance travelers describe as almost devotional. Dream-sap trees bleed a translucent sap that, tasted, brings on visions said to be prophetic rather than ordinary dreams. The forest is home to rootwraiths — said to be born from broken oaths — restless dreamstalker cats that slip between the foliage, and, somewhere in its depths, the Bloomwound Behemoth, a beast spoken of as a guardian of balance rather than a simple predator.

Echoes of the prophets Zephaniah, Habakkuk, and Elijah are said to run through the forest's roots. Locals say Eden remembers every choice made beneath its canopy, and answers — not always quickly, and not always kindly.
