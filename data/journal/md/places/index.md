# The Island by Region

Every charted place on Behel'ith, grouped by the kind of ground it is.

**Regions** are the large zones that appear on the regional map and the hunt picker. **Sites** are smaller landmarks within them -- caves, springs, standing stones -- that are known but not separately travelled to. **Sacred Mountains** have their own section.

The journal's search reads tags as well as text, so searching a biome -- `coastal`, `desert`, `wetland`, `cave`, `ruins` -- surfaces the places tagged with it, alongside anything that merely mentions the word.
