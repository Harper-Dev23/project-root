# Mount Sinai

Within the Mountains of Psalms, Mount Sinai is wrapped in thick cloud and trembling earth, lightning writing what looks like scripture in fire across its face. It is a summit no mortal is said to climb uninvited.

Sinai is regarded as the seat of unfiltered truth — divine law and an overwhelming presence given a place to stand. It is remembered as a covenant-maker, binding people to promises older than empires, and as a boundary between flesh and spirit so sharp that to hear it clearly is to risk being undone. Those brought to Sinai are said to receive divine truths that are as dangerous in their clarity as they are heavy — the mountain is said to repel liars and unmask false identities outright. The old saying holds: the mountain burned with voice, and still it waits, thundering beneath the veil.
