# The Grasping Wild

Southwest of the Forest of Eden, the Grasping Wild is a jungle of living vines that shift like serpents underfoot — paths are said to twist closed behind travelers who linger too long, and a heavy floral musk clouds judgment the deeper one goes. The vines move when unobserved, sometimes protecting a traveler and sometimes strangling them, and certain groves freeze time itself — hours spent inside pass as mere moments to anyone waiting outside. Altars strangled in vines bear the crest of King David.

Vine-walkers, beings bound entirely to the jungle's will, move through the wild alongside chimeric beasts that camouflage perfectly with the foliage, and rootbound spirits — lost adventurers long since encased in vine — linger at its edges. Local belief holds that David's final prayers still guide the vines: humility is said to calm the jungle, while arrogance wakes its wrath. Some say the Grasping Wild carries David's final heartbeat, echoing still through every leaf.
