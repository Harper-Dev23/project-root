# The Ecclesian Forest

In the north-central reaches of the island, between the Numerian Plains to the east and the Mountains of Psalms to the west, the Ecclesian Forest is made of towering, rapid-growth trees that sprout, wither, and collapse within weeks — forming a constantly shifting living architecture. Wind traces hymns through the hollow trunks as it passes. The Tabernacle Tree's hollow interior is lined with bioluminescent scripture, stone-script spires embraced by living wood bear inscriptions that rotate and change, and the Cave of the Seven Sleepers, home to seven prophetic figures locked in magical stasis, lies somewhere within its bounds.

Prophetic custodians write here with what's described as living ink, Solomonic echoes form numeric riddles in the bark, and time-lost pilgrims have been known to emerge decades older after what felt like a single night. Solomon's wisdom is said to cycle the forest's growth and decay, demanding contemplation before action from anyone who passes through. Some believe the forest is Solomon's final theorem — life itself demonstrating proof of divine recursion.
