# Camp Nehemiah

Camp Nehemiah is neutral ground, raised by all four tribes on the eastern reach of the River Wormwood, downstream from the Ruins of Exodus. It is a place of terraces, stone watch-points, and mist-ringed lakes — the only ground on the island where Hunters from rival tribes share a fire without drawing blood. Once you leave through the gate to begin a Hunt, you cannot return until it ends.

At its heart burns the Sacred Bonfire, an eternal flame around which oaths, initiations, and quiet rituals gather. Nearby stands the Waystone, a monolith — and the source of the soulbound Waystone Shard every Hunter carries — that tracks Prophet Favor and the slow opening of the island's hunt-node network. The Vendor Row and Tribe Vendor Row trade Hunt Tickets and Tribe Tickets for gear, tools, and cultural goods, while the Combat Pit lets Hunters test themselves against each other between Hunts.

Each of the four tribes — Elseth, Styx, Zafaar, and Le'sse — keeps its own leader's hut at camp, where a Hunter can speak with tribal leadership directly. Samuel Mourne, the camp's tactician, holds court in his own tent, trading in cryptic lore and the lessons of hunts gone wrong. Elder Berethos Thorne, keeper of the Hunt's law, watches over the camp's moral center, and the Seers — blind elders who speak only when stirred — keep their own quiet tent at the edges of things.

The vault's older notes also describe three nearby crescent lakes, "The Eyes," each said to reflect truth, memory, or desire, though no two accounts agree on which is which — and rumors persist of a black-market emissary trading in corrupted trophies and forbidden tools after dark. No single god claims Camp Nehemiah; it exists as a threshold, preparing those who pass through it for what waits beyond the gate.
