# The River Wormwood

Flowing east out of Lake Genesis, the River Wormwood splits in two: one branch runs south through the Ruins of Exodus toward Camp Nehemiah, the other north between the Ecclesian Forest and the Numerian Plains toward the Cradle Coast and Aurora Shores. The river is dark and slow-moving, its surface reflecting shadow but never the sky — still by day, glowing faintly violet under moonlight, with the trees and reeds along its banks all bending toward the water.

Pale vines called the Leviathan Strands drift just beneath the surface, hinting at something vast and sleeping below. Submerged statues called Stone Mouths whisper in extinct tongues during low tide, and the Split of the Sorrow-Braids marks the sacred fork where the river divides — one branch said to "weep" eastward, the other coiling north. On the northern fork, the flat Wading Stones of Augustine are said to be a resting place for the divine hippo. River-keepers touched by divine grief are drawn here, and those who drown in the Wormwood are said to sometimes return — changed. The river is believed to channel sorrow and separation, its twin branches mirroring the paths of remembrance and forgetting; legend holds it was carved by the tears of a mourning prophet who chose not to destroy, but to divide.
