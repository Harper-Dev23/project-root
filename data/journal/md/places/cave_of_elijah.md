# Cave of Elijah

High on Mount Sinai, the Cave of Elijah offers serenity, thin air, and a kind of divine illumination at sacred hours. At its heart sits the Altar of Revelation, a stone platform where Elijah is said to have once communed with the divine. The cave can only be found, it's said, by those led there by divine will, and a deep calm pervades the space — an atmosphere that soothes the spirit and is said to repel threats outright.

Elijah's spirit is believed to grant protection, inner clarity, and prophetic visions to those who rest here, often delivered through dreams or shafts of light. Local lore holds that Elijah still walks the world in whispers of wind and fire, and that the cave remains his lantern in the dark.
