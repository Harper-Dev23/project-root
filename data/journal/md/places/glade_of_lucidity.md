# Glade of Lucidity

South of the Rapids of Meribah, within the Grasping Wild, lies a bioluminescent glade filled with oversized fungi, spiral glyphs, and a resonance that feels almost alive — light here pulses like breath. The Choir Root Hive, a sentient fungal network, communicates through vibration and light, while a stone dais etched with spiraling musical glyphs hums faintly nearby. Light motes drift through the glade, pulsing in sync with any sentient mind that approaches.

Dormant echoes of the prophet Ezekiel are said to linger here, charging the air with divine resonance; vision-serpents and dream-states are said to open glimpses into lost memories or hidden truths. Local lore holds that the glade remembers every song ever sung, and that its roots once heard the whisper of a false god — and very nearly shattered.
