# The Numerian Plains

Spreading northeast beyond Lake Genesis, the Numerian Plains are golden grasslands under a vast sky of fast-moving storms and radiant sunsets. Star-aligned circles cut into the grass are said to map divine prophecy and shift with the seasons; a long trench called the Wanderer's Scar marks where a falling celestial once struck the earth, and wind-struck pillars hum to life when lightning passes overhead.

Vision-seekers read omens in the drifting clouds, riddlebeasts are said to test travelers with parables rather than claws, and Wylett caravans pass between the horizons on routes only they seem to know. The presence of the prophet Isaiah is said to linger in the storms and sky-signs here, shaping long cycles of endurance, silence, and hope. Local belief holds that the plains are where divinity once walked silently, choosing to observe rather than to command.
