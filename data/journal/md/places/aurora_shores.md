# Aurora Shores

On the northern coast bordering the Frozen Steppes, the Aurora Shores are a pristine snow beach beneath a shimmering, ever-present aurora. Crystal grains of snow refract the light overhead, giving the whole shoreline a starlit glow. Shallow melt-pools known as Falling-Star Pools are said to hold the reflections of meteors long after they've passed, prayer cairns stacked by unknown hands stay mysteriously warm, and ice arches along the shore resonate like tuning forks when touched.

Spirit elk with nebula-patterned fur are said to roam here, sky-fish leap through the aurora's waves, and hermit prophets who speak only in song are rumored to keep to themselves along the ridgelines. The heavens are said to answer silently at the Aurora Shores — travelers in meditation may glimpse moments outside of time, and false gods are said to fear the lights, which reveal the flaws in their glamours. Legend holds that on rare convergences, the aurora forms a door; those who walk through it vanish, and some return years later with starlight in their veins.
