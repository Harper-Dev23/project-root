# Dunes of Zin

Southeast of the Writhing Hills, stretching down to the southeastern coast, the Dunes of Zin are salt-laced sands torn by cyclonic winds. The sand itself is said to sing in low, mournful, almost prophetic chords, and a stone fissure known as the Cry of Nahum echoes a constant, wordless condemnation. Under rare star alignments, buried shrines are said to rise briefly from the dunes before vanishing beneath the sand again.

Bone-devouring insects haunt the deeper dunes, spirits of the sand are said to be bound here by vows they never fulfilled, and cultists are known to dig for relic shards in the ruins the dunes have swallowed. The judgment of the prophet Nahum is said to still weigh on this land — every storm here is said to carry whispers of condemned promises and broken pacts. Local legend holds that the dunes themselves are crystallized tears, wept after Nahum's final, irreversible shout.
