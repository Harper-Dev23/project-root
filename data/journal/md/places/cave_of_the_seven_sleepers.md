# Cave of the Seven Sleepers

In the heart of the Ecclesian Forest, hidden by a magical fog, lies an ancient cave that holds the Slumbering Seven — figures wrapped in robes of ages past, locked in an enchanted stasis no one has managed to explain. The walls around them are covered in prophetic carvings, long-lost languages, and divine symbolism, and a sacred altar nearby, used in rituals long since forgotten, still glows faintly with latent power.

The cave is regarded as a threshold between worlds; waking the Sleepers, it's said, could fulfill ancient prophecies — or doom them. Local legend holds that the Seven were chosen to sleep until the world called them forth once more, and that their breath, when it finally comes, will change the air itself.
