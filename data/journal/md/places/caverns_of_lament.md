# Caverns of Lament

Deep beneath the Forest of Eden lies a cool, damp cave system with smooth, bone-white walls, where gusts of air carry an eerie hissing through the narrow passages. Entrances to the caverns are scattered and half-hidden throughout Eden, and the tunnels beneath are said to run deeper than anyone has managed to map.

The caverns are home to Lamentation, a massive divine serpent bound up with grief and insight, whose presence shapes everything within. Lost travelers wander these passages consumed by sorrow, and others — described as whisper-sickened — are driven half-mad by the murmurs that echo through the stone. Those who listen closely to Lamentation's whispers are said to glimpse forbidden truths, but each revelation is said to cost a piece of the soul. Some who enter return changed; others do not return at all.
