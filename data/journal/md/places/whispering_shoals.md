# Whispering Shoals

Beneath the Writhing Hills, on the southeastern coast, the Whispering Shoals are black, sticky sands swept by warm, crimson-tinted waves. A constant susurrus of voices rides the wind here, and no footprints last — locals say the sand remembers its own and erases everyone else's. It is held to be the site of a shattered prophet's demise; water drawn from the shoals is said to spoil and weep blood within hours.

Ghost-tide pools glow faintly blue after dark, replaying fragments of the drowned, and half-buried tomb-wrecks creak as though phantom crews still walked their decks. Memory sandbars occasionally surface for a few hours, baring old relics to the air before the tide reclaims them. Tidebrood crustaceans are said to mimic the last words of the dying, the Saltworn Dead linger near drowned relics they cannot release, and pilgrims drawn here by visions sometimes fade quietly into the sea-mist and are not seen again.

Some say the shoals themselves are alive, hungering for untold stories, and that the erasure of footprints is simply the price of keeping its secrets.
