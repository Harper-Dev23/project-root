# Lake Genesis

High on the plateau above Eden, Lake Genesis is vast, impossibly deep, and ripples constantly clockwise. Mist veils the water at all hours, and light fails to reach its depths. Massive covenant stones are said to shift visibly beneath the surface, and the glowing ledges of the Glimmergrass terraces are rumored to be a site of rebirth. The King's Lave, the waterfall that feeds Lake Ezra below, begins here.

Beasts that drink from Genesis are said to be transformed, and prophets are spoken of as being reborn from its depths; sky-colored fish are said to guide pilgrims who come seeking them. The lake is regarded as the source of the creation breath itself — remaking the souls it chooses, revered and feared in equal measure. It is said to remember every name ever spoken to it in reverence, and to sing them back beneath the surface forever.
