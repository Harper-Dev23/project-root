# Lake Ezra

Within the Forest of Eden, fed by the falling waters of the King's Lave, Lake Ezra is mirror-clear and gleams with a faint inner light, its surface rippling now and then with a current that seems to move on its own. The lake's reflections are said to show deeper truths — sometimes even futures — to those who look long enough. Seasonless trees along its banks bear fruit that, eaten, brings on serene visions, and spirit-bound deer are known to kneel at the shoreline at twilight.

Lake Ezra is regarded as a gift of sustenance and peace from Solomon and David; drinking from it in a contemplative state is said to heal both body and heart. Locals say its ripples are the breath of the Creator Prophet, exhaled forever in hope.
