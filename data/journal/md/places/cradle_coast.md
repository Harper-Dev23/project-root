# Cradle Coast

Beyond the Numerian Plains on the northeastern shoreline, the Cradle Coast is a stretch of quiet dunes where reeds seem to sing lullabies to the breeze, and iridescent shells shimmer with shifting light. Stone cradles, partly buried in the sand, are sized strangely — for infants, or perhaps for prophets — and glowing entities known as the Spiritlight Processions are said to drift along the tideline at dawn and dusk. The surf itself carries a kind of tidal harmony, said to fall into rhythm with a listener's own heartbeat.

Reverent beasts are known to kneel here at sacred dawn, prophet-marked children have been known to simply appear along the coast, and ancestral spirits are said to drift as blue wisps along the dunes. The Cradle Coast is held to be the site of the prophet Micah's birth-energy — a place that grants beginnings rather than endings, where first cries are said to echo forward into destiny. One legend tells of a night when time itself paused here, so that a love could be born without sorrow and without blood.
