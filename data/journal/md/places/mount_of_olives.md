# Mount of Olives

Within the Mountains of Psalms, the Mount of Olives is a quiet, moonlit rise above sacred ruins, where olives fall in the places tears once did, and time feels thinner than it should.

The mountain is remembered as a place of sorrow and insight — those who come here to mourn are said to see more clearly than most. It is held to be a veil between worlds, a place where divine sorrow slips into mortal dreams, and a site of both return and departure: departure for gods, return for mortals. Prophetic dreams and final farewells are said to echo here, and rituals of grief or forgiveness performed on its slopes are believed capable of shifting fate itself. As the old line goes: he wept, and then the stars bent down and carried him up.
