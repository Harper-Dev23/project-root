# The Abyssal Shores

At the edge of the Frozen Steppes, on the north-northwest coast, the Abyssal Shores are a frozen seascape where gentle waves hide deadly, glass-clear ice spires beneath them. The air is eerily still, and sound seems to vanish into the cold. Transparent ice towers called Crystal Fangs pierce ship hulls without warning, moon-mirror crevasses reveal submerged marble ruins only when the full moon's reflection lines up just right, and silent floes of ice drift and spin with no wind to move them.

Apparitions of sailors trapped in the ice are said to linger along the shore, translucent predators phase beneath the floes, and a rumored spectral leviathan is said to cause ice tremors with the beat of its own heart. The shores are believed cursed by an exiled divine messenger — compass needles spin wildly here, and prayers are said to freeze mid-air before they can be heard. Local legend holds that the ice spires are the teeth of a sleeping ocean god, and that every shipwreck only feeds its dream.
