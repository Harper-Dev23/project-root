# Prophet's Throat

Beneath Mount Ararat's western cliffs, hidden under tangled roots and salt-laced fog along the Wicked Coast, a yawning sinkhole drops away into helical layers of brine-laced air, phosphorescent scripture, and the echoes of voices long since drowned. The place smells of old copper and wet stone, and water here never evaporates. The Spiral Descent, a natural shaft coiled like a nautilus, is wide enough for a whale to fall through, slick with salt and algae the whole way down. Deeper still lies the Cathedral of Bubbles, a submerged cavern where rising bubbles form coherent speech for anyone able to hear submerged prophecy, and the Prophet's Rib, a hollow vertebra-shaped mineral formation that endlessly rebounds anything whispered into it.

The Throat is held to be home to Yar'galeth, a false god known as the Submerged Mouth — a site of inverted revelation, where truth loses its meaning by arriving too early, too often, or in the wrong form. Visions received here are said to always be "true," but never in context; speaking while submerged is believed to let Yar'galeth hear, and respond. The prophet Daniel's network of prophecy is said to have once passed through this space, before it was corrupted by echo bleed and narrative loops.

Local lore holds that what was first spoken here was too perfect — even the prophets sealed it away — and that when a truth drowns, it becomes prophecy. Some say that if you shout your purpose into the Throat, it returns the words in another tongue: a truer one, or a deadlier one.
