# The Writhing Hills

East of the Forest of Eden and northwest of the Dunes of Zin, the Writhing Hills are violet sands under a constant heat-haze, rolling like sleeping serpents. The ground shifts subtly underfoot, and the air feels dry, charged, and uneasy. Dunes called the Shadowfall Ridges are shaped like coiled serpents and are said to hide secret paths, the Whisper Slopes hum with sounds like half-heard arguments, and ribbons of obsidian called Glass Veins are said to reflect past violence rather than a traveler's present form. From the northwest ridge, the stone spires known as the Judges can be seen casting unnatural shadows across Lake Genesis in the distance.

Unnamed entities, rumored to sense regret, are said to move through the hills, and travelers can be consumed by the shifting guilt-patterns that seem to follow them. The hills are believed to hold a kind of buried judgment — remembering sins and reshaping paths to match them. Legend holds that these dunes once coiled around the body of the First Serpent, and that they still twitch whenever a lie is spoken too close.
