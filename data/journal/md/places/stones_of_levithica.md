# Stones of Levithica

At the heart of the Ruins of Exodus stand the Stones of Levithica: ten monumental pillars arranged in a perfect circle, each etched with faint runes that glow softly indigo when touched — warm beneath a pious hand, cold beneath a false one. The arrangement resists every attempt at translation, though some scholars insist it forms a celestial cipher rather than a simple monument.

Fragments of the ritual once tied to the stones suggest it required a name, a blood offering, and the silence of a full moon, though the full rite has long since been lost. At midnight, the circle is said to light up — but only nine of the ten stones ever respond. The tenth glows for no one. Some claim the stones were built to protect something, though no two stories agree on what; others believe the circle was meant as a warning that was never finished being written.
