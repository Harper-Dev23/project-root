# The Mire of Plagues

Below the cliffs of the Mountains of Proverbs along the western coast lies the Mire of Plagues — a stagnant rot-swamp of divine infection, fungal towers rising above murky pools choked with insects and fetid mist. At its center sits the Spill, a crater-lake of divine contamination, surrounded by Flesh-Blister trees that ooze corrosive sap and bog-lanterns that lure wanderers straight into sinkholes.

Plagueborn beasts, mutated by the rot, share the swamp with rot-cultists preaching decay and forsaken prophets ranting through fevered delusions. The mire is believed to have been warped by false gods — echoes of the prophet Nahum's scream are said to produce a spiritual sickness in the very air. Local lore calls the Mire a divine wound that never scabbed over, its infection a lesson written out in decay.
