# The Mountains of Psalms

North of Lake Genesis, forking into northwest and northeast arms with the Ecclesian Forest at their eastern edge, the Mountains of Psalms are mist-veiled peaks that resonate with worship — echoes here are said to weave naturally into a kind of sacred choir. Amphitheaters called the Echoing Sanctuaries return any song sung within them as a blessed echo, glowing mineral seams known as the Hallelujah Veins pulse at certain sacred hours, and the Psalmic Stair is said to shift its verses on each step to match the devotion of whoever climbs it.

Hymn-scribes are said to etch music directly into the stone, spirit-song birds mimic divine melodies through the passes, and echo-prophets are said to appear only during moments of pure praise. The harmonies of David and Elijah are believed to resonate in every stone here, and the names of false gods are said to dissolve when sung into the cliffs. Legend holds the mountain stone is frozen music — on holy nights, the cliffs are said to hum with soundwaves visible to the eye.
