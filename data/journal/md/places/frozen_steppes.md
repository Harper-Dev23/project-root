# The Frozen Steppes

Far to the north, beyond the arms of the Mountains of Psalms and Proverbs, the Frozen Steppes are endless pale tundra under razor-cold winds, where footfalls vanish into a devouring silence. Pools of perfectly smooth ice called the Still Wells reveal scenes that, by all accounts, should not be visible at all; soundless cairns topple and always return no matter how many times they're knocked down, and lattices of black ice called the Black-Ice Veins consume light and warmth wherever they spread.

Frost-marked beasts of primal will roam the steppes alongside devout wardens of Micah and Amos, while emissaries of false gods are said to attempt dominion over the same frozen ground. The Steppes are regarded as a frozen battlefield between Micah's justice, Amos's warning, and the rising ambition of false gods — a spiritual equilibrium that cracks audibly under the icy pressure. Local legend holds that Micah once froze a false god's curse mid-word, and that the ice still trembles with the power of that unsaid defiance.
