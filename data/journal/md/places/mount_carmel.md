# Mount Carmel

Within the Mountains of Psalms, Mount Carmel is rocky and scorched — the site of an ancient conflict whose marks never quite healed. Ravens circle overhead, and the ground still splits in places where fire once fell.

Carmel is remembered as a site of trial by fire: a place of divine confrontation, where falsehood is called out and cannot hide. Lies are said to melt away here, leaving only raw belief behind, and only those with a true calling are said to survive what the mountain demands of them. When invoked, Carmel becomes a battlefield for truth — magic tied to deceit fails, and divine forces are said to clash in storms visible for miles. The old line about it still holds: there He answered by fire, and the liars turned to ash.
