# Cave of Adullam

Hidden deep in Eden's ancient forest, shrouded by dense foliage and sacred stillness, the Cave of Adullam is cool, damp, and easy to miss — its camouflaged entrance is obscured by heavy growth and watched over by the region's wildlife. Inside, sacred carvings line the walls, etched symbols that seem to emanate a quiet, reverent energy, leading deeper to the King's Chamber, a central sanctuary said to hold the spectral presence of King David himself.

David's lingering spirit is said to bless those who enter with reverence, offering protection, wisdom, and spiritual strength to anyone judged worthy. Local lore holds that David once fled to this cave in a time of need, and that he watches still — lending strength to the desperate and the devout.
