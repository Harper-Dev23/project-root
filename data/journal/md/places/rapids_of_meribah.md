# Rapids of Meribah

Southwest of Lake Genesis, the Rapids of Meribah cut through winding ravines filled with violent currents — a perilous route that leads, eventually, to a hidden bioluminescent glade. The currents demand great caution to cross, the ravine's jagged walls form deadly natural choke points, and clusters of glowing mushrooms along the banks mark the approach to whatever lies beyond.

A subtle divine energy is said to guide travelers toward the glade, resisting easy navigation as though testing the worthy. Local tribes warn that the waters carry memory, and that those who disrespect them are pulled under by what the river remembers.
