# The Mountains of Proverbs

Running along the western spine of the island, curling from west of Lake Genesis north toward the Frozen Steppes, the Mountains of Proverbs are bleached cliffs etched by wind into shapes that look almost like scripture. The silence here is thick enough, travelers say, to echo a person's own thoughts back at them. A stone stair called the Path of Correction is said to grow warm beneath the feet of anyone walking it with a falsehood in their heart, and the inscribed Testament Pillars are said to shift to reflect whatever truth a person carries inside. Time-distorting caves along the slopes are used by penitents seeking contemplation.

Silent pilgrims undergo rituals of ego-shedding here, stone-bound beasts are said to punish arrogance, and wind-voices ask riddles sharp enough to cut at the soul. The mountains are held to be Solomon's wisdom forged directly into rock — a place that strips away illusion and leaves only raw truth. Local myth holds that the cliffs themselves were formed when a lie too heavy to bear cracked the horizon open.
