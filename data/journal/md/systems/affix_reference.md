# Affix Reference (In Depth)

Every affix that can roll on gear, with its tier and exact range. For the short version, see [[Items & Equipment]].

## How many affixes roll

| Rarity | Affixes |
|---|---|
| Common | none |
| Uncommon | 1–2 |
| Rare | exactly 3 |
| Epic | 4 — always 2 prefixes + 2 suffixes |

Prefixes and suffixes are drawn from separate pools. **Unique** items ignore all of this and carry one fixed bonus instead.

## Tiers

Most affix families have three tiers. **T3** is the common, weakest band; **T1** is the strongest. Tier is baked into the affix name — *Sanctified* is always the weakest Max HP prefix, *Elijah’s* always the strongest — so a name tells you the band before you read the number.

One family goes further: **weapon damage %** has a fourth band, **T0**, held by *Merciless* alone at 28–40%. It is the single largest affix in the game.

## Handedness scaling

A two-handed weapon rolls the canonical ranges below. One-handers are reduced, because they can be paired:

- **Per-swing** affixes (flat and % weapon damage, flat elemental) roll at **2 / 3** of the listed range.
- **Globally additive** affixes (buildup %, elemental/necrotic %, healing %) roll at **0.5**.

Shields are exempt — they carry no damage budget to balance against.
