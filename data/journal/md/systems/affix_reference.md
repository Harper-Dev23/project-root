# Affix Reference (In Depth)

Every modifier that can roll on gear, with its tier and exact range.

Which of these an item can actually roll depends on its **item level** — see
[[Item Level & Base Types]] for the gating. For the short version of what
modifiers are, see [[Items & Equipment]].

## How many roll

| Rarity | Affixes |
|---|---|
| Common | none |
| Uncommon | 1–2 |
| Rare | exactly 3 |
| Epic | 4 — always 2 prefixes + 2 suffixes |

Prefixes and suffixes are drawn from separate pools. Two modifiers from the same family can never appear on one item, so you will not see two Strength suffixes or two tiers of the same buildup. **Unique** items ignore all of this and carry one fixed bonus instead.

## Tiers

Every family runs **five tiers**. **T5** is the common, weakest band; **T1** is the best. The tier is baked into the name, so the name tells you the band before you read the number. Which tiers an item can roll is decided by its **item level** — see [[Item Level & Base Types]].

## Handedness

Two-handed weapons roll the ranges below. One-handers are reduced, because they can be paired:

- **Per-swing** modifiers (flat and % weapon damage, flat elemental) roll at **two thirds**.

- **Globally additive** modifiers (buildup %, elemental/necrotic %, healing %) roll at **half**.

Shields are exempt — they carry no damage budget to balance against.

### Suffixes — hybrid

A hybrid builds **two** weakness families at once, for roughly 62% of what a single-family modifier of the same tier would give to one. Breadth costs magnitude, so it is never a straight upgrade. Pairs only ever form inside one category. A hybrid and a matching single-family modifier can both appear on the same item.
