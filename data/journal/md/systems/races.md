# Playable Races

A Hunter's race does two things: it shifts their starting attributes, and it decides how they move across the field.

Every race grants a **net +6** across the six attributes. What differs is the shape — where the points land, and what each race gives up to get them.

Movement is a **bonus action**. Three races take a short, frequent step; three cover more ground but need longer to recover.

## The Six

### Human -- *Tactical Reposition*
**+1 to every attribute.** Moves 1 tile, every 2 turns.

No spike and no gap. Humans begin a little further along in every direction than anyone else, and a little behind in none.

The most numerous people alive, and the ones the Hunt was invented for -- every one of the four tribes is a human nation sending champions.

> "We move, we live. That's how it's always been." -- Samuel Mourne

### Dwarf -- *Stone Step*
**+2 STR, +3 CON, +2 WIS, -1 DEX.** Moves 1 tile, every 2 turns.

The most durable opening a Hunter can take, bought with accuracy.

A mountain people and builders by trade, who grade their own work by how hard the stone was. A few ride with Zafaar.

> "Stand fast and let the storm break around you."

### Elf -- *Feystride*
**+2 DEX, +3 INT, +2 CHA, -1 CON.** Moves 1 tile, every 2 turns.

Sharp-minded and well-regarded, and thinner than most.

Thinkers, from the northern island and the northern twin continent, where standing and intellect are coveted above nearly all. More varied than outsiders assume -- see [[Peoples of the World]].

> "A brush of wind, a turn of the shoulder, and death passes by."

### Ferrow -- *Gust Shift*
**+2 DEX, +3 WIS, +2 CON, -1 STR.** Moves 2 tiles, every 3 turns.

Precise and hard to wear down, at the cost of raw force.

Nomadic explorers of high places, bright-feathered and endlessly varied in pattern, split between those who hunt by day and those who hunt by night.

> "The air remembers."

### Wylett -- *Briar Dash*
**+1 DEX, +3 WIS, +3 CON, -1 CHA.** Moves 2 tiles, every 3 turns.

The most resilient of the six against weakness buildup, and among the slowest to seize a turn.

Survivalists found wherever the world is thriving, carrying both prey and predator lines. Some travel with Le'sse.

> "Cornered, even the smallest beast bares its fangs."

### Skith -- *Scale Slide*
**+3 DEX, +3 INT, +1 STR, -1 CHA.** Moves 2 tiles, every 3 turns.

Sharp and accurate, with the same reluctance to act first.

Of desert, jungle and sea. Cutthroats and pragmatists, with little patience for the distinction. Some travel with Styx.

> "Strike once, regret twice."

## A note on movement

The named moves differ only in reach and recovery -- a 1-tile step every 2 turns, or a 2-tile bound every 3. The name belongs to the race; the ground covered is the same for everyone who shares it.
