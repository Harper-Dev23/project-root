# Awakenings: Grunt

A Grunt who undergoes the Awakening chooses **one** of three paths. The choice is permanent, and the other two close.

Each path is a constellation of twelve nodes — three entry, four adept, four master, one capstone — against only **eight** points earned across levels 3 to 10. No path can be filled; every one of them forks. See [[Levelling and the Awakening]].

## Brute

*The Fist — A dense, compact cluster rather than a long branching path.*

Force applied directly. Heavy hands, broken guards, and being the last one standing.

| Tier | Nodes |
|---|---|
| Entry | Heavy Hands, Thick Skin, Iron Grip |
| Adept | Unstoppable, Crush, Bull Rush, Brace for Impact |
| Master | Immovable, Break the Guard, Last One Standing, Bone Breaker |
| Capstone | **Mountain's Fury** |

## Berserker

*The Forked Bolt — A lightning bolt that forks partway up into two jagged branches, reconverging at one strike point.*

Momentum through pain. Wounds ignored, pulse quickened, fury that feeds on its own damage.

| Tier | Nodes |
|---|---|
| Entry | First Blood, Red Vision, Quickened Pulse |
| Adept | No Retreat, Reckless Swing, Adrenaline, Ignore the Wound |
| Master | Blind Fury, Second Wind, Unchecked, Blood Frenzy |
| Capstone | **The Endless Rage** |

## Adorned

*The Gem — A faceted diamond outline; branches trace the facet lines.*

Craft worn as armour. Stones set, runes inlaid, light refracted into something that guards you.

| Tier | Nodes |
|---|---|
| Entry | First Facet, Rough Cut, Polished Edge |
| Adept | Runic Inlay, Sympathetic Resonance, Set Stone, Refracted Light |
| Master | Layered Facets, Resonant Chain, Hardened Setting, Prism Guard |
| Capstone | **The Flawless Cut** |

*The Awakening is not yet implemented. These paths can be previewed from the Talents tab on the level-up screen, but none can be undergone and no node can be spent — the names and shapes are settled, the effects are not yet written.*
