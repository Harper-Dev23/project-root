# Combat Basics

Battles are turn-based, fought between your party and a set of enemies. Each side fills three positional columns — **front, mid, and back** — and some skills can shift a unit between them, advancing toward the front or retreating toward the back.

Status effects — the nine Weakness families (Lightning, Cold, Fire, Disorient, Lacerate, Expose, Disease, Curse, Toxic) — stack up through combat actions and shift the fight's pace: slowing turns, draining resources, or wearing down defense over time. See the Weakness System Overview for the full tier breakdown.

Hunt encounters route into this same combat system — fights triggered during a Hunt (Beasts or Cultists) play out in the Combat Pit exactly like any other battle, with no way to flee once engaged.
