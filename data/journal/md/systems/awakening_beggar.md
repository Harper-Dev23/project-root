# Awakenings: Beggar

A Beggar who undergoes the Awakening chooses **one** of three paths. The choice is permanent, and the other two close.

Each path is a constellation of twelve nodes — three entry, four adept, four master, one capstone — against only **eight** points earned across levels 3 to 10. No path can be filled; every one of them forks. See [[Levelling and the Awakening]].

## Brigand

*The Broken Chain — A chain of linked nodes routing around one snapped link.*

Getting out clean. Quick fingers, back alleys, and the knot slipped before anyone notices.

| Tier | Nodes |
|---|---|
| Entry | Quick Fingers, Light Step, Loose Lips |
| Adept | Back Alley, Cut and Run, Slip the Knot, Second Pocket |
| Master | Vanishing Act, Marked Target, Silent Take, Broken Lock |
| Capstone | **King of Thieves** |

## Harbinger

*The Comet's Eye — A comet breaking apart with a watching eye at its core.*

Dread as a weapon. Omens read aloud, panic spread, ruin announced before it arrives.

| Tier | Nodes |
|---|---|
| Entry | The Sky Is Falling, First Warning, Cracked Omen |
| Adept | Cold Reading, It Was Foretold, Panic in the Streets, Whispered Doom |
| Master | Prophet of Ruin, Written in Ash, The Watching Eye, Fate Sealed |
| Capstone | **The End Is Written** |

## Rogue

*The Junk Heap — An irregular, deliberately asymmetric scrappy cluster.*

Improvisation. Scrap, salvage and jury-rigging — making do with whatever the last fight left behind.

| Tier | Nodes |
|---|---|
| Entry | Make Do, Rig the Trap, Pocket Full of Nothing |
| Adept | One Man's Trash, Scrap Blade, Jury-Rig, Improvised Guard |
| Master | Salvage Run, Booby Trap, Second-Hand Steel, Patchwork Guard |
| Capstone | **Waste Not** |

---

*The Awakening is not yet implemented. These paths can be previewed from the Talents tab on the level-up screen, but none can be undergone and no node can be spent — the names and shapes are settled, the effects are not yet written.*
