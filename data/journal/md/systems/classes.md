# Base Classes

A Hunter's base class grants a **net +3** across the attributes and one **class skill** -- an ability drawn from its own action pool, so using it never costs a Major or Bonus action.

Every class begins knowing a Basic Attack and its own racial movement skill. Nothing else is granted at creation — every other ability comes from the weapon in your hands.

## The Six

### Grunt -- *Blockade*
**+2 STR, +1 CON.** The front line. Raises a wall the enemy has to deal with before reaching anyone standing behind it.

### Acolyte -- *Blessing*
**+2 WIS, +1 CHA.** The devoted healer. WIS drives both their resilience and how much their healing actually restores.

### Scholar -- *Meditate*
**+2 INT, +1 WIS.** Restores a spent bonus action and shakes off effects that would otherwise hold them in place.

### Beggar -- *Hide*
**+2 DEX, +1 CON.** Drops out of sight from the back row. Breaking cover with an attack lands it with far better accuracy -- but taking a hit first ends it for nothing.

### Shepherd -- *Watch Over*
**+2 WIS, +1 DEX.** Places their attention on a single ally and answers what happens to them.

### Performer -- *Transpose*
**+2 CHA, +1 DEX.** Alone among the six in carrying three versions of its class skill -- Fire, Lightning and Cold -- redirecting elemental buildup rather than dealing it.

## Choosing

Every class gives the same **+3** total, and every race the same **+6** — so whatever pairing you choose, a Hunter begins with exactly **+9** on top of the attributes set at creation. No combination is quietly ahead of another. A class is picked for its skill and the role that follows from it, not for a numerical edge.
