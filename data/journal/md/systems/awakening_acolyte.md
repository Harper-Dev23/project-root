# Awakenings: Acolyte

A Acolyte who undergoes the Awakening chooses **one** of three paths. The choice is permanent, and the other two close.

Each path is a constellation of twelve nodes — three entry, four adept, four master, one capstone — against only **eight** points earned across levels 3 to 10. No path can be filled; every one of them forks. See [[Levelling and the Awakening]].

## Empath

*Linked Rings — Two overlapping circles; shared nodes sit in the overlap.*

Carrying weight. Pain shared, burdens borne, suffering moved from one body to another.

| Tier | Nodes |
|---|---|
| Entry | Shared Pain, Open Wound, Gentle Touch |
| Adept | Borrowed Strength, Mirrored Wound, Echo of Others, Steady Presence |
| Master | Carry Their Weight, Bear the Burden, Radiating Calm, Bound Suffering |
| Capstone | **One and All** |

## Crusader

*The Sword — A cross-hilted blade: a wide guard of stars above, narrowing down the blade to a single point.*

Judgment carried by hand. Sigils cut into steel, sentence passed without argument, and the hush that comes before it lands.

| Tier | Nodes |
|---|---|
| Entry | Sworn Witness, The Hush, First Sentence |
| Adept | Sigil-Cut Steel, Weight of Judgment, Unflinching, Banner of the Just |
| Master | Cleave the Stone, No Warning, Inflexible Wrath, Enforcer's Mark |
| Capstone | **The Sentence Falls** |

## Sage

*Standard Tree — No strong constellation motif — a plain branching tree: three roots up into a trunk, out to branches, capstone at the top.*

Study. Verses recited, margins annotated, meaning found on the second and third reading.

| Tier | Nodes |
|---|---|
| Entry | First Verse, Steady Study, Quiet Vigil |
| Adept | Recite the Rite, Living Scripture, Second Reading, Marginal Note |
| Master | Hidden Verse, Deep Study, Old Testament, Annotated Page |
| Capstone | **The Final Chapter** |

---

*The Awakening is not yet implemented. These paths can be previewed from the Talents tab on the level-up screen, but none can be undergone and no node can be spent — the names and shapes are settled, the effects are not yet written.*
