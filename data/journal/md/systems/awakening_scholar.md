# Awakenings: Scholar

A Scholar who undergoes the Awakening chooses **one** of three paths. The choice is permanent, and the other two close.

Each path is a constellation of twelve nodes — three entry, four adept, four master, one capstone — against only **eight** points earned across levels 3 to 10. No path can be filled; every one of them forks. See [[Levelling and the Awakening]].

## Ritualist

*Concentric Circles — Nested rings, unlocked outward-in.*

Preparation. Circles drawn in advance, rites layered and repeated, working that deepens the longer it runs.

| Tier | Nodes |
|---|---|
| Entry | First Sigil, Chalk Line, Steady Hand |
| Adept | Bind the Circle, Echoing Rite, Widen the Ring, Second Casting |
| Master | Layered Rite, Borrowed Time, Circle Within Circle, Deepened Working |
| Capstone | **The Final Working** |

## Elementalist

*The Compass — A four-pointed cross radiating from a center hub.*

Breadth over depth. Fire, ice and storm held at once, balanced and shifted between rather than mastered singly.

| Tier | Nodes |
|---|---|
| Entry | First Spark, Tempered Ice, Storm's Breath |
| Adept | Stone Skin, Wildfire, Rolling Thunder, Undertow |
| Master | Elemental Balance, Elemental Shift, Twin Elements, Eye of the Storm |
| Capstone | **Convergence** |

## Alchemist

*The Flask — Fills bottom-up like liquid, capstone at the bottle neck.*

Measure. Doses, ratios and reactions — a path about getting the proportions exactly right.

| Tier | Nodes |
|---|---|
| Entry | First Formula, Steady Hands, Measured Dose |
| Adept | Volatile Compound, Refined Reagent, Catalyst, Distillation |
| Master | The Long Boil, Balanced Ratio, Reactive Mix, Saturation Point |
| Capstone | **The Perfect Formula** |

---

*The Awakening is not yet implemented. These paths can be previewed from the Talents tab on the level-up screen, but none can be undergone and no node can be spent — the names and shapes are settled, the effects are not yet written.*
