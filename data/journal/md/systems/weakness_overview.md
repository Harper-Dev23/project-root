# Weakness System — Overview

This journal page summarizes the **nine weakness families** and their T1/T2 names and identities.

| Family | ID | T1 | T2 | Core Identity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lightning | `lightning` | Zapped | Shocked | Flat damage jolts; T2 can proc multiple jolts |
| Cold | `cold` | Chilled | Frostbitten | Slows turn order; weakens output/defense |
| Fire | `fire` | Singed | Ablaze | Flammable actions; start-of-turn burn |
| Disorient | `disorient` | Dazed | Concussed | Increased costs; drains MP |
| Lacerate | `lacerate` | Bleeding | Hemorrhaging | Actions cause buildup; %HP loss at turn start |
| Expose | `expose` | Raw | Flayed | Reduced physical defense; crit vulnerability |
| Disease | `disease` | Sickened | Plagued | Attrition and recovery penalties |
| Curse | `curse` | Hexed | Afflicted | Spiritual debilitation and synergy hooks |
| Toxic | `toxic` | Poisoned | Envenomed | Stacking poison damage over time |

Use this as a quick reference; detailed values and scaling live in the full Weakness System doc.
