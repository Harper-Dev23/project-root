# Awakenings: Shepherd

A Shepherd who undergoes the Awakening chooses **one** of three paths. The choice is permanent, and the other two close.

Each path is a constellation of twelve nodes — three entry, four adept, four master, one capstone — against only **eight** points earned across levels 3 to 10. No path can be filled; every one of them forks. See [[Levelling and the Awakening]].

## Bonded

*Binary Star — Two orbiting points feeding a shared central spine.*

Partnership. Nearly every node concerns a second party — sharing wounds, matching pace, watching flanks.

| Tier | Nodes |
|---|---|
| Entry | First Whistle, Calloused Hands, Steady Grip |
| Adept | Shared Wound, Second Set of Eyes, Loyal Stride, Matched Pace |
| Master | Pack Leader, Guard the Flank, Read the Wind, Old Scar |
| Capstone | **Twin Hearts** |

## Warden

*The Shield — A ring with radiating spokes, like a shield boss.*

Holding ground. A wall of a path: bracing, unbroken lines, and refusing to give way.

| Tier | Nodes |
|---|---|
| Entry | Raise the Crook, Steady Stance, Watch the Gate |
| Adept | Circle the Flock, Turned Blade, Brace and Hold, Line Unbroken |
| Master | Shepherd's Wall, Hold the Line, Iron Resolve, Last Stand |
| Capstone | **Nothing Gets Through** |

## Primalist

*Claw Marks — Three jagged diagonal slashes converging downward.*

The animal underneath. Teeth, hunger, and standing between the flock and whatever is coming.

| Tier | Nodes |
|---|---|
| Entry | Bare the Fangs, First Growl, Hackles Raised |
| Adept | Between Them and You, Blood for Blood, Snapping Jaw, Guard Dog |
| Master | The Old Hunger, Broken Leash, Feral Guard, No Mercy |
| Capstone | **The Wolf Remembers** |

---

*The Awakening is not yet implemented. These paths can be previewed from the Talents tab on the level-up screen, but none can be undergone and no node can be spent — the names and shapes are settled, the effects are not yet written.*
