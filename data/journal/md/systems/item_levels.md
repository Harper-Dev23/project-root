# Item Level & Base Types

Two hidden numbers decide what a piece of gear *could* have been: its **item level** and its **base type**. Neither is a stat. Both are stamped when the item is made and never change afterwards.

For the modifiers themselves, see [[Affix Reference (In Depth)]].

## What item level decides

Item level never changes a number directly. It decides two things: which **modifier tiers** were allowed to roll, and which **base types** were in the pool. Rarity is a separate axis entirely — it sets how *many* modifiers roll, not how good they are.

Gating is a floor and never a ceiling: a high item level does not stop the weak bands rolling, it only adds better ones on top and makes them steadily more likely. The weakest tier remains the single most common outcome at every level.

## Base types

Weapons run **Crude → Hardened → Ancestral**, armour runs **Simple → Fitted**. A higher base carries more raw damage (or, on armour, one more stat) before any modifier is applied, so every percentage on the item multiplies a bigger number.

| Base tier | Weapons | Armour | Available from item level |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Crude | Simple | 1 |
| 2 | Hardened | Fitted | 2 |
| 3 | Ancestral | — | 8 |

## Where item levels come from

| Source | Item level | Base types |
|---|---|---|
| Encounters I–III | 1 | tier 1 only |
| Encounters IV–V | 2 | tier 1 only |
| Encounter VI | 3 | tier 1 only |
| Any Reckoning tier | 3 | up to tier 2 |
| Bone pile — Hunt Ticket | your party's level | tier 1 only |
| Bone pile — Reckoning Mark | your party's level (min 3) | always tier 2 |

The two bone-pile buttons are deliberately fixed: a Hunt Ticket always buys a tier-1 base and a Reckoning Mark always buys a tier-2 one. A shop should say what it sells.

## Bone

Bone is not a base type of its own. It is an **overlay** on whatever base was already going to drop, adding **+20% damage** before any other modifier — which means every percentage on the weapon multiplies the bone bonus too. Because it sits on top of a normal base, Bone can appear on any base tier.

Every tier reads simply *Bone Dagger*, *Bone War Mace*. The Base Tier line on the item is what tells the three apart.
