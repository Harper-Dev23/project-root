# Awakenings: Performer

A Performer who undergoes the Awakening chooses **one** of three paths. The choice is permanent, and the other two close.

Each path is a constellation of twelve nodes — three entry, four adept, four master, one capstone — against only **eight** points earned across levels 3 to 10. No path can be filled; every one of them forks. See [[Levelling and the Awakening]].

## Virtuoso

*The Lyre — Vertical strings plucked in sequence, building to a chord at the top.*

Building to something. Rhythm kept, tempo raised, and a final movement everything else was leading toward.

| Tier | Nodes |
|---|---|
| Entry | Perfect Pitch, First Chord, Steady Rhythm |
| Adept | Crescendo, The Standing Ovation, Harmonic Resonance, Encore |
| Master | Final Crescendo, Perfect Harmony, Rising Tempo, Final Movement |
| Capstone | **Symphony of War** |

## Jester

*The Hat — Three points rising off a shared brim — the side points are cheap, self-contained specializations; the center is the longer route to the capstone bell.*

Keeping it all in the air. Three points of a hat, two of them cheap and self-contained, one long route to the bell.

| Tier | Nodes |
|---|---|
| Entry | Keep 'Em Up, Steady Hands, First Toss |
| Adept | Quick Catch, Double Toss, Sleight of Blade, The Big Finish |
| Master | Crowd Pleaser, Blind Throw, Perfect Timing, Triple Cascade |
| Capstone | **Never Drop the Act** |

## Animancer

*The Marionette — A central puppet node with strings running up to controller nodes.*

Strings and stagecraft. Puppets raised, cast filled, and a bow taken at the end.

| Tier | Nodes |
|---|---|
| Entry | Cut the Strings, First Puppet, Loose Joint |
| Adept | The Show Must Go On, Double Act, Tangled Strings, Second Puppet |
| Master | Final Bow, Full Cast, Puppet Swarm, Tangled Fate |
| Capstone | **Master of the Stage** |

---

*The Awakening is not yet implemented. These paths can be previewed from the Talents tab on the level-up screen, but none can be undergone and no node can be spent — the names and shapes are settled, the effects are not yet written.*
