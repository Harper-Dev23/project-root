# Basic Training II

_Six reinforced dummies that shuffle around — practice range and AOE shapes._

The same rack, rebuilt on rollers. These are sturdier — twice the hit points — and, critically, they **move**. Each carries a shuffle that shifts it one column sideways, and they will use it to slide out of ground hazards they are standing in.

## How the fight works

Still no incoming damage. What changes is that the board no longer holds still for you.

- **Shapes have to be aimed at where a target will be**, not where it is. An area effect placed on a dummy's current square is a wasted action if it shuffles out before resolution.
- **Ground hazards get actively dodged.** The dummies treat hazard squares as something to leave, which is the first time the pit pushes back on a "drop a zone and forget it" habit.
- **Column matters.** A sideways shuffle changes which of your ranks can reach a given dummy, so a target that was in range at the top of the round may not be by the time your turn comes around.

## What it teaches

This is the AOE-shape trainer. Every custom shape in the game is described relative to ally and enemy slot numbers, and this encounter is where you learn to read those shapes on a board that is actually changing. Practise the ones you intend to rely on here, where a miss costs nothing.

After this, the pit stops being polite — [[The Animated Six]] hit back.
