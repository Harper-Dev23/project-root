# Gorrek

_Gorrek — a relentless berserker who punishes hesitation and tears open his own wounds for power._

The pit's final fight, and a solo boss. Everything before this was about reading a formation; Gorrek is about reading **one** opponent who changes state as the fight goes on.

## How the fight works

- **He punishes standing still.** His glare is a free action that marks a target and demands they move by the end of their next turn — ignore it and it takes a substantial slice of maximum health. It costs him no action economy to threaten you with it.
- **His initiative builds fast**, and his self-buffs accelerate it further. Falling behind on the gauge compounds.
- **He feeds on his own wounds.** He carries lifesteal, and his healing scales up as his health falls — at the edge of death he recovers meaningfully more from every point of damage he deals. A slow grind at low health is exactly the wrong plan.
- **Death Spiral is gated.** It needs the target carrying **both** Expose and Lacerate at their second tier before he will commit to it. That gate is your warning: if you are letting both meters run, you are choosing to be hit by it.
- **He tears himself open deliberately.** Part of his kit applies Lacerate to *himself* in exchange for power, which is why leaving his bleeding unaddressed is a real decision rather than a free win.

## What it teaches

Action economy under pressure, and that weakness meters cut both ways. Gorrek is the exam for everything the pit has taught.

## The Reckoning

Beating him unlocks a rematch ladder. Each tier is account-wide and gates the next, and the design lever is **toughness first** — HP, resists and Resilience climb steadily while his damage stays deliberately restrained. His kit only grows at marked tiers, so the ladder is about your damage output holding up over a longer fight rather than about surviving new one-shots.

## Damage typing

Overwhelmingly physical. Physical Resist and Resilience are the defensive stats that pay here.
