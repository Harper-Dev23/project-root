# The Pit and Its Occupants

Everything recorded here is a fight you can actually take, written down after the fact. Two kinds of entry live in this section.

**Encounters** describe a specific engagement: who is on the field, how they work together, and what the fight is trying to teach you. Read these before walking in.

**Adversaries** describe a single combatant in isolation — their profile and their complete ability list. The same adversary can appear in any number of encounters, so these are written once and referred to from wherever they turn up.

## How to read an entry

Each entry is split. The upper half is written by hand: the synopsis, the reasoning, what to watch for. The lower half is **transcribed directly from the combat records** — rosters, health, resistances, and every ability with its action cost, resource cost, cooldown and effect.

That lower half is not maintained by hand and does not drift. If an ability is retuned, the entry reflects it the next time the records are transcribed. Where a number here disagrees with your memory of a fight, trust the number.

## What is deliberately absent

Internal tuning values are not recorded — the dials used to balance an encounter are not knowledge a combatant would have. What you get is what you could learn by fighting the thing and paying attention: what it does, what it costs, and how long it takes.

## The training pit

Six encounters, arranged as a curriculum rather than a difficulty ladder:

| | Encounter | Teaches |
|---|---|---|
| I | [[Basic Training I]] | Targeting, reach, the shape of a turn |
| II | [[Basic Training II]] | Area shapes against a moving board |
| III | [[The Animated Six]] | Focus fire; breaking a supported formation |
| IV | [[Huntsman & Beasts]] | Priority targeting; damage types actually mattering |
| V | [[Elemental Duelists]] | Weakness meters as a liability, not just a resource |
| VI | [[Gorrek]] | Action economy under pressure, plus a rematch ladder |

Fights beyond the pit — beasts met on the hunt, rival parties, whatever the cult fields — will be recorded here as they are survived.
