# Basic Training I

_Six stationary dummies for safe sparring._

The pit's first rack. Six straw-and-lacquer figures on fixed posts, weighted so they rock when struck and settle upright again. They do not move, do not target, and cannot deal damage — the only ability any of them has is a sway that exists so the turn order has something to advance past.

## How the fight works

There is no fight. Every dummy has 10 HP and no offence whatsoever, so the encounter runs entirely at your pace. It exists to let you confirm three things without any clock on you:

- **Targeting** — that you can select a specific figure in a specific slot, including ones behind others.
- **Reach** — which of your abilities can touch the back rank from where you are standing, and which cannot.
- **Resolution flow** — the shape of a turn: major action, bonus action, class action, and what each one consumes.

Six bodies across the field is deliberate. It is enough to make rank and column matter visually before anything is at stake.

## What it teaches

Positioning is not cosmetic. Which slot you occupy determines what you can reach and what can reach you, and this is the only encounter in the pit where you can learn that with no consequence for getting it wrong.

Move on to [[Basic Training II]] once targeting feels automatic.
