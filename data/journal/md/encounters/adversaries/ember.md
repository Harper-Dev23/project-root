# Ember

Half of the [[Elemental Duelists]], and the Fire side of their engine.

Ember drives Fire buildup, but the point is not Fire alone — it is the contrast with [[Rime]]'s Cold. Their cross-element payoffs scale on the target carrying buildup in the *opposing* family, so Ember's job is partly to make Rime's abilities hit harder.

If Rime dies first, Ember enrages: attack power, crit and Resilience all climb, and an ability unlocks that was previously unavailable.

**Watch for:** killing the other duelist and assuming the fight got easier.
