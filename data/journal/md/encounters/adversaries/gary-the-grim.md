# Gary the Grim

The curse caster of [[The Animated Six]], and the pit's early source of necrotic pressure.

Gary works in layered affliction rather than raw output — curses, needles, and damage that bypasses physical mitigation entirely. He fights with the same tactical depth a real caster would, holding abilities for the moment they pay off rather than repeating one spell.

**Watch for:** stacking Physical Resist and assuming you are defended. Against Gary specifically, Necrotic Resist is the stat doing the work.
