# Shifty-Eyed Mo

The most annoying construct in the pit, and the one most people misread.

Mo is reactive: he answers the instant an ally is struck. The important detail is *when* his answer lands. His curse resolves **before your attack rolls**, so it weakens the very strike that triggered it — not the next one. Players who assume it is a normal debuff consistently under-account for it.

**Watch for:** your damage coming in lower than expected on the swing right after you hit one of his allies. That is Mo, not variance.
