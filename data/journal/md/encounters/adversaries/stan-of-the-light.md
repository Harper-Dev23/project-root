# Stan, of the Light

Genuine healing magic, running genuine healing logic.

Stan does not cast blindly. He filters to allies who are actually wounded, prefers the lowest-health target among them, and will not spend a cast on someone already at full health. His cleanse behaves the same way — it only fires on allies carrying real weakness buildup, never on an unafflicted target.

The practical consequence is that **spread chip damage is close to worthless** against his formation. Damage dealt evenly across six constructs is damage Stan can comfortably undo. Concentrated damage that threatens to actually kill something is what forces him to react.

**Watch for:** the moment he runs low on resources. He regenerates a small amount each turn, so a long fight favours him.
