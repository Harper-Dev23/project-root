# Oskar

The ripper of [[Huntsman & Beasts]] — the heaviest body on that field.

Oskar is a brute: high health, physical output, and a straightforward threat profile compared to his packmate. What complicates him is rot. His infection and decay abilities carry a necrotic component, so he is not a pure physical problem even though he mostly reads as one.

**Watch for:** assuming the big melee beast is fully answered by Physical Resist. Part of his kit is not.
