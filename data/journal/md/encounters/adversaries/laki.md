# Laki

The owl that joins [[Huntsman & Beasts]] only once its Reckoning ladder starts, and the pit's one dedicated **Disorient** engine.

Where Oskar and Kiro press on health and damage type, Laki attacks your *turn* — Disorient's first tier raises what your skills cost, and its second drains the Initiative Gauge outright. Almost her entire kit builds it: a screech that spreads it across the whole party, a bonus action that applies it for no damage at all, and a reaction that answers being struck with more of it. Left alone she does not kill you; she makes everything else in the fight cost more.

Her one real strike is conditional on that work. Silent Dive hits harder against a target already Rattled, and harder still against one that is Reeling — so the pressure she builds is also what sharpens her.

She is also the hardest thing on the field to hit. She starts with substantial Evasion and can nearly double it on herself for three turns, which makes spending a big cooldown on her a genuine gamble.

**Watch for:** the reaction. Hitting her at all builds Disorient on whoever swung, so the character grinding her down is the one who ends up paying the most for their skills.
