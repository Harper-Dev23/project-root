# Kiro

The venom-spewer of [[Huntsman & Beasts]], and the pit's clearest **Necrotic Resist** test.

Kiro's kit is built on venom, and venom in this game is the Toxic family — whose own tier damage is necrotic. His abilities convert most or all of their damage to necrotic accordingly, from pure spat venom and gas clouds through to bites and swipes that carry a real physical component alongside the acid.

The practical consequence: a build stacked entirely into Physical Resist takes close to full damage from him. Necrotic Resist cuts it sharply.

**Watch for:** his reactive venom. It answers being hit and carries no physical component at all.
