# Doug Longshot

The ranged reactive of the formation.

Doug answers when an ally is struck, adding damage from the back rank. He is less individually dangerous than [[Shifty-Eyed Mo]] but contributes steadily, and being in the back rank means a fair number of melee approaches cannot reach him at all without repositioning first.

**Watch for:** treating the back rank as safe to ignore. Doug's contribution accumulates across a long fight.
