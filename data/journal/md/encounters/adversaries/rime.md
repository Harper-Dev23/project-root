# Rime

Half of the [[Elemental Duelists]], and the Cold side of their engine.

Rime drives Cold buildup and carries Cold's own punishing side effects — reduced evasion and reduced outgoing damage on anyone whose meter climbs. Paired with [[Ember]]'s Fire, the two of them are stacking opposed meters on you specifically because their kits pay out on the contrast.

If Ember dies first, Rime enrages on the same terms: a real power spike, plus a newly unlocked ability.

**Watch for:** letting Cold climb quietly. Its penalties are not damage, so they are easy to miss until your accuracy and output have both quietly degraded.
