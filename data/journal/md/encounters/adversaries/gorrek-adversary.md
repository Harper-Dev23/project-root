# Gorrek, the Berserker

The berserker at the end of the pit. See [[Gorrek]] for the full encounter, including the Reckoning ladder.

Gorrek's profile is deliberately lopsided: enormous health, heavy physical resistance and Resilience, and a damage output held on a tight leash by design. He is built to be a long fight rather than a lethal one — the danger is attrition and his own escalating recovery, not burst.

Three things define him mechanically:

- **Desperation healing.** All healing he receives, lifesteal included, scales up as his health falls — up to half again at the brink. Grinding him down slowly is the worst available plan.
- **Free action pressure.** His glare costs him nothing and threatens a large slice of your maximum health if you do not move.
- **Self-inflicted Lacerate.** Parts of his kit wound him deliberately in exchange for power, so his own bleeding is a resource he is spending rather than a problem you have given him.

**Watch for:** letting both Expose and Lacerate reach their second tier on one of your characters. That is the gate on Death Spiral, and it is the single largest hit in his kit.
