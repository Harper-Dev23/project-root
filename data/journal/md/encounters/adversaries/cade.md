# Cade

The commander of [[Huntsman & Beasts]], and the encounter's real centre of gravity.

Cade deals comparatively little damage himself. What he does is **make his beasts' damage land**: marking targets to draw focus, applying Expose buildup, and handing the pack Initiative and Accuracy. His strongest pack buff is conditional — it only unlocks once a target already carries two or more active weaknesses — so his contribution escalates as the fight goes on and your meters fill.

**Watch for:** the temptation to fight the things that are hurting you. The beasts are the damage; Cade is the reason it connects.
