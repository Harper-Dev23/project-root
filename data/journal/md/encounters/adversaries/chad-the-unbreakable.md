# Chad the Unbreakable

The wall of [[The Animated Six]], and the reason precision targeting fails against that formation.

Chad's defining trick is **redirection**: he can pull an incoming attack onto himself before the roll resolves, meaning a carefully aimed strike at a fragile back-rank construct simply lands on him instead, at his defences rather than theirs. He pairs that with self-buffs and a rallying call that hardens the whole group.

**Watch for:** committing your biggest single hit into what looks like an opening. If Chad is standing and healthy, assume he can take it for someone else.
