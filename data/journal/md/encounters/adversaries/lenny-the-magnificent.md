# Lenny the Magnificent

The artillery of [[The Animated Six]].

Lenny channels a large area effect that has to be answered — left alone, it hits the whole party at once. He is fragile relative to his output, which makes him the classic focus-fire target, and precisely the target [[Chad the Unbreakable]] exists to protect.

**Watch for:** the channel. Once it starts, you are choosing between interrupting it and eating it.
