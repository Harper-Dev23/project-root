# Elemental Duelists

_Battle elite fire and ice duelists leveraging elemental reactions._

Two combatants, opposed elements, and the pit's only fight built on **cross-element interaction**. [[Ember]] and [[Rime]] are not two separate damage sources — they are one combined engine, and each half makes the other's payoff bigger.

## How the fight works

- **They build opposing meters on purpose.** Ember drives Fire buildup, Rime drives Cold. Neither is trying to reach a threshold alone; they are stacking *both* on you, because their kits pay out on the contrast between them.
- **Thermal Shock.** Once a target carries buildup in the opposing family, their cross-element abilities scale up. Letting both meters climb is strictly worse than letting either one climb alone.
- **Killing one enrages the other.** Both carry an on-ally-death rage: the survivor gains attack power, crit and Resilience, and unlocks an ability it could not previously cast. Dropping one duelist early does not halve the fight — it converts the remainder into something meaner.

That last point inverts the usual advice. Focusing one down is normally correct; here it hands the survivor a power spike. Either bring them down together, or be certain you can finish the second before the rage matters.

## What it teaches

Weakness meters are not a one-way accumulator you can ignore. This is the encounter where letting a meter fill is actively punished, and where Resilience — the only stat that actually reduces buildup accrual — earns its slot.

## Damage typing

Almost entirely elemental. Elemental Resist is the stat that matters here; Physical and Necrotic mitigation are close to dead weight.
